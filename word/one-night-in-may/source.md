> Spotted: Deftones' 2027 dates, with San Antonio on a Thursday in May.

They played the same building in March 2025. This time they're bringing a hardcore band and a shoegaze band.

## Here's the date

Deftones announced the run Thursday, November 12, 23 dates from May 5 to June 20, 2027. San Antonio is Thursday, May 6, at Frost Bank Center. Live Nation lists it at 7 PM, but the venue still says TBA.

Knocked Loose and julie open the headline dates. Knocked Loose is hardcore from Oldham County, Kentucky, and their last album was You Won't Go Before You're Supposed To, in 2024. julie is a shoegaze trio from Los Angeles. Their debut, my anti-aircraft friend, came out in September 2024.

The tour's behind private music, the band's 10th album, from August 2025. It debuted at No. 5 on the Billboard 200.

## So here's how you get in

The artist presale is Tuesday, November 17, 10 AM to 10 PM. The Frost Bank Center and Live Nation presales are Wednesday. General sale is Thursday, November 19, at 10 AM on Ticketmaster.

## What you heard and what's on the record

You heard three different deadlines for the presale signup. CultureMap says midnight Monday. The venue's page says Sunday at 12 AM. Billboard says 10 PM local.

Here's the signup page itself, which closes November 16 at 1 AM Eastern, and in San Antonio that's midnight, the second Sunday turns into Monday. So do it Sunday and stop doing math.

## But May's already loud

Saturday, May 1, Meshuggah plays the Boeing Center at Tech Port with Testament and Blood Incantation. Thursday, May 6, it's Deftones. Saturday, May 22, Metallica plays the Alamodome with Limp Bizkit and Avatar, one night only.

That's three metal shows in San Antonio in 22 days. Pace yourself.

The rest of Texas gets Deftones twice. Dallas is Wednesday, May 5, at Dos Equis Pavilion. Then Saturday, May 8, it's the Cynthia Woods Mitchell Pavilion in The Woodlands, which the press keeps calling Houston.
