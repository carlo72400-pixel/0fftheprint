You know Miku. This is the other one. The one that started as a lie.

## Here's what's coming

Beebom had it on November 5. Kasane Teto headlines Fortnite Festival Season 16, and it starts Thursday, November 12. She gets her own Music Pass. Her shop items show up on November 14. Beebom says she'll also be in Battle Royale as a character you can walk up to.

She takes the stage from Lil Tecca, who had Season 15. So a rapper is handing the mic to a voice library.

## But she was a joke first

In March 2008 a bunch of people on 2channel, the Japanese message board, built a fake Vocaloid. They voted on her details in a thread. On April 1 they put her on Niconico as the next release from Crypton, the company behind Hatsune Miku. It was an April Fools joke. There was no product.

Her official site still says it flat. She isn't a Vocaloid and she was never Crypton's.

Then the joke got a voice. One of the people who made her recorded her own, and it went into UTAU, a free singing program where anybody can build a voice. In April 2023, 15 years in, she got a paid one called Synthesizer V AI Kasane Teto.

## So read her profile

It's on the official site and it's all still the bit. Age 31. Species, chimera. Favorite thing, French bread. Thing she's bad at, singing.

That's the headliner. Her own bio says she can't sing.

You heard there are skins, songs and a price. Not yet. Beebom says Epic's sharing the rest later this week. The one extra detail is a leak. It's a helper that puts a bubble around you for 15 seconds, and a leak is still a leak.

We think that's the real story. Nobody signed her. Nobody owns her the way a label owns a singer. A message board made her up, and people kept writing songs for her till a game with a main stage had to call.
