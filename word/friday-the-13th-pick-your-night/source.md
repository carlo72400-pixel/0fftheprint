## Here's Friday

San Marcos. BLOODBATH is back for year 4 at Private Park. The host posted it on November 3. Doors at 7, bands at 8, DJs till 2. $10. 18 and up, 21 and up to bring your own. The tag on the post tells you to wrestle at your own risk, and she says she's making the blood herself, by the bucket. It's fake. The address is on her flyer, not here. We shot WAP in that yard in September.

San Antonio. The Kings of Horror Convention opens at Wonderland of the Americas, the mall on Fredericksburg Road, and runs through Sunday. It's the third one. It has a whole Friday the 13th guest table, with C.J. Graham, Thom Mathews and Darcy DeMoss. Advance tickets are $10. The con's own site gives two start times for Friday, 3 and 5. Show up at 5 and you're right either way.

Austin. DIVALI is at New Guild Co-op, 8 to 2, hosted with One Bee Presents. Six names are on the post.

Anywhere. Victorian Psycho opens Friday from Bleecker Street. Zachary Wigon directed it and Maika Monroe plays a governess who shows up at a gothic manor. So people start to wonder about her. We couldn't find a San Antonio showtime as of Monday, so check your theater.

## But Saturday's stacked too

11 to 5, Saturday and Sunday, TAYO NA launches its third issue at the Millennium Youth Entertainment Complex in Austin. It's a magazine about Austin's Filipino artists and makers, and the launch comes with a two-day indoor market and a gallery. It's free. The page says about 200 free parking spots.

6:30 to 9, The Down Market puts on The Creative Mass II at Legacy Park in downtown San Antonio. Poetry, live music, art and DJs. Street Lamp's tour post puts their San Antonio date there.

9 PM, KTSW and KVRX throw a Battle of the Bands at Pearl Street Co-op in Austin. Five bands, and Blue Navy goes last at 12:15. [Both of those bands have their own story today](https://0fftheprint.com/word/both-bands-left-town/).

And the con's still open.
