> Spotted: Molly Santana in The Fader on November 5, signing to Epic under her own imprint.

In July her tour disappeared. In November she's got a label inside a label.

## Here's the deal

Epic Records announced it on Thursday, November 5. Molly Santana signed, and her next album comes out through Black Punk Music. That's her own imprint, and it sits under Epic. She's 22.

She told Billboard the imprint is a new chapter for her. The first single under the deal came out the next day. It's called "A Girl Is a Gun."

## But look at her summer

On June 16 she and North West announced the Kimokawaii Tour. 14 dates. Two headliners. Opening August 5 in Dallas.

Then on Thursday, July 30, Complex caught every date marked cancelled on Ticketmaster and the tour's site offline. That's 6 days before the first show. North West confirmed it that night. Nobody's put a reason on the record, so we're not guessing one.

## So what's an imprint

It's a small label that lives inside a big one. The big one usually pays for things and gets the record into the apps. The small one belongs to the artist, at least by name.

It's not a new move. 21 Savage did it at this same label, with Slaughter Gang, in January 2017. NLE Choppa took No Love Entertainment to Warner in July 2019, when he was 16. Polo G started ODA with Columbia in September 2020.

So she's next in a line. But she's also been on the other side of it. In June The Fader said she'd just left her deal with Victor Victor and gone independent again. Five months later she's back at a major, and this time her own imprint is on the door.

## What you heard and what's on the record

You heard she owns her masters. Nobody's said that. The Fader doesn't say who owns what, and Billboard's piece is behind a paywall. What's on the record is the deal, the imprint, one single and one guess. The Fader thinks the new singles sound harder than her last album. That last album made its list of the best of 2025.

Also on the record. She's on "Ran to Atlanta" with Drake and Future. That was her 2026 before any of this.

## So mark your own calendar

There's no Texas date. The only two she had this year were Dallas on August 5 and Houston on August 6, and both went down with the tour. San Antonio and Austin weren't on that list at all.

So the thing to watch for is a new tour. If the Texas stops come back the same way, Houston's about 3 hours from San Antonio.
