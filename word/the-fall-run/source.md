Texas is about to be loud for two straight months. Here is the run, so nobody says they did not know.

## September

Sep 1, Dos Equis Pavilion, Dallas. Grey Day rolls through with $uicideboy$ and Destroy Lonely on the bill. A Tuesday, which is very Grey Day of them.

Sep 15, Moody Center, Austin. Ken Carson brings the xperimenting tour, and the openers are the actual story: xaviersobased and prettifun on the same stage. If you have been waiting to see the underground get a room that size, this is the night. Same tour hits Irving the next evening for the ones who cannot make Austin.

Sep 17 through 24, Alamo Drafthouse South Lamar, Austin. Fantastic Fest. Thirty five world premieres, a Zach Cregger Resident Evil, a Carrie, and a closing night that ends in a GWAR concert, which is the most honest way any film festival has ever ended.

## October

Oct 13, Germania Amphitheater, Austin. Grey Day again, Destroy Lonely again. The tour knows where its people live.

Oct 24, Texas Motor Speedway, Fort Worth. Sick New World. System of a Down, Deftones, Slayer playing Reign in Blood front to back, Evanescence, Prodigy. And buried in the undercard, the two names the day is secretly for: Boy Harsher and Twin Tribes. The darkwave is coming to a speedway. Nobody plans these things, they just happen to Texas.

> Pick two, not four.

That is the honest advice. The stretch from Oct 13 to Oct 24 is stacked on top of festival season, and every one of these rooms will still be standing next year. The house will be in the ones it can reach, cameras up. If your show belongs on this list and is not, the DMs are open.
