Texas is about to be loud for two straight months. Here's the run, so nobody says they didn't know.

## September

September 1, Dos Equis Pavilion, Dallas. Grey Day rolls through with $uicideboy$ and Destroy Lonely on the bill. It's a Tuesday. Very Grey Day of them.

September 15, Moody Center, Austin. Ken Carson brings the xperimenting tour. But the openers are the actual story, xaviersobased and prettifun on the same stage. If you've been waiting to see the underground get a room that size, this is the night. Same tour hits Irving the next evening, for anyone who can't make Austin.

September 17 through 24, Alamo Drafthouse South Lamar, Austin. Fantastic Fest. 35 world premieres, a Zach Cregger Resident Evil, a Carrie, and a closing night that ends in a GWAR concert. That's the most honest way any film festival has ever ended.

## October

October 13, Germania Amphitheater, Austin. Grey Day again, Destroy Lonely again. The tour knows where its people live.

October 24, Texas Motor Speedway, Fort Worth. Sick New World. System of a Down, Deftones, Slayer playing Reign in Blood front to back, Evanescence, Prodigy. But buried in the undercard are the two names the day's secretly for. Boy Harsher and Twin Tribes. The darkwave's coming to a speedway. Nobody plans this stuff. It just happens to Texas.

> Pick two, not four.

That's the honest advice. October 13 to October 24 is stacked right on top of festival season, and every one of these rooms will still be standing next year. We'll be in the ones we can reach, cameras up. If your show belongs on this list and isn't on it, the DMs are open.
