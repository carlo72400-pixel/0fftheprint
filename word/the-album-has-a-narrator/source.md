> Spotted: Pirouette on every app Wednesday, told through the eyes of something called One.

In October we wrote about the cover. [Yoshitaka Amano drew it](https://0fftheprint.com/word/the-vampire-painter-drew-2hollis/). Now there's a record under it.

## Here's the record

Pirouette came out Wednesday, November 11, on Interscope. It's the fifth 2hollis album, 16 songs in about 48 minutes.

His note to fans says it's told through the eyes of a being called One. Dork, Clash and Stereogum all ran with that. We couldn't find him explaining it anywhere. In a September interview with i-D, he said the mystery comes from not over-explaining. So he didn't.

He produced most of it himself. Skai, Ginseng, Andrew Aged and Mk.gee added production, and Stereogum says Mk.gee co-wrote "Human After All."

## What you heard and what's on the record

In October you heard Playboi Carti and Ken Carson were on it. We said nobody had announced a single feature. Now it's out, and Apple credits all 16 songs to 2hollis alone.

That rumor traces back to a September write-up saying he'd teased work with both of them. It never said this album.

## But read the reviews

Pitchfork gave it a 7.1 on Thursday. NME gave it 3.5 out of 5 and called "Daisy" the high point. Dazed ranked "Daisy" first too. FLOOD singled out "Pressure" and "Daisy."

"Daisy" has a history. Dazed says it floated around unreleased in 2024 and got pulled from streaming before it ever came out. Now it closes the album.
