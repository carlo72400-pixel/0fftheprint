Ken Carson's bringing the Xperimenting Tour through Texas twice in September. Austin on the 15th at the Moody Center. Irving on the 16th. Neither one is San Antonio, and there's no San Antonio date on the whole run.

That's not a complaint. It's just how the run is shaped. The tour goes August 26 to September 23, and the album it's carrying is Xperiment, out July 3 on Opium and Interscope. It's his fifth, with Playboi Carti, Destroy Lonely, Lil Uzi Vert and Young Thug on it. A record that size plays the rooms that hold 20,000 people, and this city is a drive from two of them.

## Leave early for the opener

The support is xaviersobased, Prettifun and DJ Moon.

xaviersobased is the one. He's been the most interesting person in that whole post Opium lane for two years, and he did it from a bedroom and a phone. The photo at the top of this page was taken at Baby's All Right, a room in Brooklyn. Going from a room like that to a 20,000 seat arena in Austin isn't a small jump. You want to have seen it before it stops being surprising.

Openers get soundchecked into a half full room while people are still parking. Don't do that to this one.

## Two dates. Both drivable.

Austin's 80 miles. Irving's a little under four hours, which is a day, not an evening.

If you're picking one, pick Austin on the 15th. It's the closer room and the bigger room. It's also two days before Fantastic Fest opens on the same side of town, so the 15th through the 17th stacks up fast if you've got somewhere to sleep up there.
