THE RUN

Get There Early

Ken Carson takes the Xperimenting Tour to Austin and Irving in September, skips San Antonio entirely, and puts xaviersobased on first.
0TP-016  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  SEP 02, 2026

xaviersobased at Baby's All Right, Brooklyn, February 2023, by Ninecalling, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0. Cropped.

Ken Carson brings the Xperimenting Tour through Texas twice in September. Austin on the fifteenth at the Moody Center, Irving on the sixteenth. Neither of those is San Antonio, and there is no San Antonio date on the run at all.

That is not a complaint, it is just the shape of it. The tour is 26 August to 23 September, the album it is carrying is Xperiment, out 3 July on Opium and Interscope, his fifth, with Playboi Carti, Destroy Lonely, Lil Uzi Vert and Young Thug on it. A record that size routes through the rooms that hold twenty thousand people, and this city is a drive away from two of them.

The reason to leave early is the opener

The support is xaviersobased, Prettifun and DJ Moon.

xaviersobased is the one. He has been the most interesting person in that whole post Opium lane for two years, doing it from a bedroom and a phone, and the jump from a Brooklyn room like Baby's All Right, which is where the photograph at the top of this page was taken, to a twenty thousand seat arena in Austin is not a small jump. It is the kind of thing you want to have seen before it stops being surprising.

Openers get soundchecked into a half full room while people are still parking. Do not do that to this one.

Two dates, both drivable

Austin is eighty miles. Irving is a little under four hours, which is a day, not an evening.

If you are picking one, pick Austin on the fifteenth. It is the closer room, it is the bigger room, and it is two days before Fantastic Fest opens on the same side of town, which means the fifteenth through the seventeenth is a genuinely absurd stretch of week if you have somewhere to sleep up there.
