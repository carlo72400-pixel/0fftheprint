> Spotted: PULSE ANGELS 3 at 21st Street Co-op, Saturday, October 3.

15 DJs. 3 stages. Till 2 in the morning. This is a dorm.

A week before that, we shot a five-band show in the same building. A couple weeks before that, BRAINROT at New Guild, another co-op a few blocks over. We think the best rooms in Austin right now aren't clubs. They're dorms. So we looked into how that works.

## It was built for this

21st Street opened in November 1974. An Austin architect named William Tamminga designed it and built it. When it opened, it was dark red cedar with bright blue trim. So it wasn't an apartment somebody turned into a co-op later. It was built to be one.

100 people live there. Upstairs there's a room called the Commons, and touring and local bands play it.

Now here's the part that breaks our brain. This isn't a venue. It's somebody's home. A private room is $1,080 a month, and that includes cooked meals. The rent comes with a job, too. Everybody works up to 6 hours a week. So the people throwing the party are the same people mopping the floor the next morning.

## But the other house is older

1936. A group of guys at UT start the Campus Guild, the first student co-op in Austin. By 1972 their building's condemned. So on August 31, 1972, they hand the lot to the Inter-Cooperative Council for $10,000, and the council buys a former women's dorm for $120,000. They call it the New Guild. Rent's $90 a month.

Then on July 4, 1973, the old Guild burns down.

So New Guild is basically what's left of the first one, and it kept partying, because the May Day Red Ball stayed a New Guild tradition until the late '80s. Today it's the council's biggest house. 32 people. Its own page calls it an artistic-bohemian commune.

## Nobody here is the boss

So why do the shows keep ending up in somebody's house instead of a club? Because nobody here is the boss. At 21st Street, even the rent gets voted on by the members. In 2014 the Austin Chronicle gave the house a Best of Austin for Best "Whoever Holds the Conch." That's a Lord of the Flies joke. It's also just accurate.

## So the house says yes

A night happens when a crew brings it and the house says yes. XBONGX came in with One Bee. Five bands on a Saturday in somebody's living situation. PULSE ANGELS came from The New Project, a creative collective out of UT, with Groove Sanctuary and ithertz. We think that's the whole difference. A club books whatever sells drinks. A house books what the house wants to hear.
