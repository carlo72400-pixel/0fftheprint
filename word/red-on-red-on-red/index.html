THE NIGHT

Red on Red on Red

A vendor market at five and a burlesque show at seven at the Rah Rah Room, shot in the hardest light in the city and printed four ways.
0TP-022  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  SEP 14, 2026

The Rah Rah Room does this thing where the afternoon and the night are two different businesses in the same building. At five it's a vendor market under the skylights. Tarot pulls and spell candles for $10. Hand painted skate decks. Oddities. A diorama with a sign reading POPULATION: WUN, which turned out to be WUN MOR's table. So somebody from our own roster was selling out of a booth before the show. By seven the tables are gone, the red velvet curtain is lit red, and it's a burlesque night.

Six acts

Feather fans. A green corset and long gloves. A cape that came off to black lingerie. Red on red on red. A headdress showgirl. A corset with garters. An MC in between who kept the room exactly as loud as it needed to be. Every act got its own clip and its own stack of frames pulled from the video, chosen for the pose, not for the pixels.

The room's the hardest lighting problem in the city, and it isn't close. A red wash on red velvet is close to monochrome. On five of the eight stage clips, the green and blue channels sit at 1 to 5 percent of the red. A face detector finds nothing, because there's no color to find. So we judged how readable every frame was on its red channel alone, the only one carrying any information. Then we built the grades to keep the red rich and pull it from orange toward crimson instead of letting it go flat.

Four prints

RED VELVET is the room's own grade. Red kept rich. Magenta and violet louder for the LED tables. Gold in the sequins and the spotlight. Halation on the lights. INSOMNIA is the movie grade. We tried 15 looks, and it's the only one that goes cool on this room without clipping a single frame. Then two dark ones, made on purpose the morning after. OXBLOOD leans into the red. Wine and gold and a black floor. OXWULF crosses OXBLOOD with the look of Robert Eggers' Werwulf. Color film graded to read like orthochromatic stock, partly blind to red, muted, cold in the shadows, with black and white grain over the color. But the reds survive as dusty wine instead of dying to gray.

We retouched every portrait in Photoshop, on real layers, before the grain. So the flash portraits in front of the curtain read soft and dramatic instead of hot. We left the stage frames exactly as lit, because that lighting is the performer's and it was already right.

Where it lives

Forty four moments from the afternoon and the show are up, each in all four grades, full quality, free to download. The act clips are up too. If you were on that stage or at one of those tables, you're probably in one of them.

Rah Rah Room, San Antonio. Sunday, September 13, 2026, twenty past five until nine.
