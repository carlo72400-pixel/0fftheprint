The Item Shop Is The New MTV

MTV's first video was "Video Killed the Radio Star." This October the drops came through Fortnite, Street Fighter before its movie, Yeat, and now A$AP Rocky.
0TP-043  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  OCT 21, 2026

The Fortnite Pro-Am at E3, Los Angeles, June 2018, by Sergey Galyonkin, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0. Cropped.

Spotted: A$AP Rocky teasing his own Fortnite skin on his Instagram Story this week.

You used to wait for the video. Now you wait for the skin.

Here's this month

On October 10, Ryu and Chun-Li from the new Street Fighter movie showed up in the shop. A week later the movie opened #1 with $30.8 million. So the skin came out before the movie did.

On October 15, Yeat got his own Icon skin and his own Jam Tracks. Find five bells in the game and it hands you another one, HeliMan, for free.

Fortnitemares runs till November 1. Freddy Krueger, Five Nights at Freddy's, Hollow Knight, Hazbin Hotel, The Purge and Obsession are all in it.

But it started with a concert

February 2, 2019. Marshmello played a set inside the game and about 10.7 million people were there at the same time. April 23, 2020. Travis Scott's Astronomical hit 12.3 million at once, and 27.7 million people caught one of the five shows. In August 2021 it was Ariana Grande.

Then it stopped being a one-night thing. Fortnite Festival launched December 9, 2023, with The Weeknd as its first Music Icon. In April 2025 Sabrina Carpenter got her own season, with "Juno" and "Nonsense" in the pass.

So here's the MTV part

MTV went on the air August 1, 1981, at 12:01 a.m. The first video it played was "Video Killed the Radio Star." 45 years later the radio star has a pickaxe.

We think that's where music lives now. A label doesn't just drop a video anymore. It drops a skin, an emote and a song you play yourself, in a game people already have open. That's not a promo. That's the release.

What you heard and what's on the record

You heard Rocky's season starts November 1. That's still a guess. Beebom says the pirate mini-season is expected November 1, and Epic hasn't said a word. HotNewHipHop called it an apparent collab. Here's the part we'd watch. LiveLoveA$AP turns 15 on October 31. If the guess holds, his skin shows up the next morning.

So mark your own calendar

Fortnitemares ends November 1. If you want Freddy, get him before then. No drive time on this one. It's on your phone.
