The album came out on a Tuesday. The party was the Monday before it, at Slackers, which is a sports and arcade bar, which means the room was lit by arcade cabinets and fifteen televisions and nothing else, and the colour changed every time somebody put a quarter in something.

Mike Dimes is San Antonio's rapper. Not a rapper from San Antonio, the one the city points at. My Story went everywhere in 2021, Texas Boy came out on Epic in 2023 with Denzel Curry and Wiz Khalifa on it, and B.I.L.L.Y is the new one: fifteen tracks, out 2 September, Maxo Kream, 1900Rugrat and Ski Mask The Slump God on the features, and darker than anything he has put out. The night before it dropped he threw a party in a barcade and wore a Miley Cyrus Bangerz tour shirt to it.

## The room

A claw machine, a pool table, dartboards, a shooter cabinet with the light guns still on their cords, a DJ table set up between the cabinets with a laptop covered in stickers, and a bar at the back with a wall of tequila. Nobody had to be told where to stand. People played the games, people stood at the DJ table, people leaned on the pool table with a drink and watched him work the room.

He worked the room. Every frame of him is a frame of him with somebody else in it: grinning at a guest, laughing over the DJ's shoulder, throwing hand signs above a crowd with a pink-haired girl looking up at him, the gold MIKE DIMES medallion swinging on the chain. Later the shirt came off and the chain stayed on.

## Two grades, same night

The room was the problem and the solution. On-camera flash reaches whoever stands closest and falls off behind them, so in a crowd of eight faces the front one is bright and the back one is a silhouette. Every face was brought up on its own, one at a time, so a group shot reads as a group and not as one person and seven shadows. The arcade screens lit every frame a different colour, so the frames lit by a single colour had the missing channels rebuilt until the people in them stopped being outlines.

Then two prints. HERO is the clean one: dense blacks, contained highlights, skin that looks like skin, the photograph first and the mood second. CHARACTER is the loud one: crushed blacks, hot whites, every machine in the room allowed to keep its own light. Open any frame in the gallery and tap between them.
