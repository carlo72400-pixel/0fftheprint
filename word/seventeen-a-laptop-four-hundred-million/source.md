In January 2022 a seventeen year old uploaded a short film to YouTube about a place with no exits. He made it in Blender, at home, about a thing the internet had invented a few months earlier on an image board.

In May of this year A24 put his feature in theatres. It cost under ten million dollars and it has taken close to four hundred million worldwide. The domestic opening weekend alone was eighty one and a half million.

He is twenty.

## Why this one matters and the other ones do not

Every year somebody gets plucked off the internet and it usually means nothing, because the thing that got them noticed gets sanded off on the way to the theatre. That is not what happened here. The film is a hundred and ten minutes, it stars Chiwetel Ejiofor and Renate Reinsve, and it sits at eighty seven percent with three hundred critics.

The audience score is the interesting split. CinemaScore came back a B minus, which for a horror film that made four hundred million is not a failure, it is a signature. It means the thing that people came for is also the thing that some of them could not sit inside for two hours. Dread with no exit does that.

## The part for anybody holding a camera

He did not go to film school first. He did not wait for permission, or a grant, or a producer, or somebody to tell him the idea was allowed. He made the short version of the thing over and over on a laptop until the short version was undeniable, and the industry came to him.

That is the whole lesson and it is not a comforting one, because it means the only real move is to keep making the thing. Nobody was going to find him if the videos were not already good.

The production design on the feature was still built in Blender, which is free.
