The plan was Club 212 on the River Walk. The room was dead, so the night moved. Thursdays do that. Up to the 212 rooftop first, where the string lights and the downtown skyline do most of the work. Then north to Bar House on North Alamo. Then one last dark blue LED club at ten to three in the morning.

212 College Street is worth a line on its own. It's one building with more than one room in it. Club 212 is upstairs, and we shot MIGX in that room five days earlier. Same address, different night, different floor, completely different photo. Bar House is the opposite kind of place. It's a three level bar built into a hundred year old house on North Alamo, with a rooftop deck pointed at the skyline, multicolor paneling inside and a DJ on the weekend.

Two cameras went out. A Luna Ultra for the portraits, and a wide action camera for the rooftop. That's why a handful of frames are a fisheye of the table with the photographer in them. Both shot RAW, and we graded both from the RAW.

## What a bar hop asks of a grade

Nothing in a night like this repeats. A rooftop under string lights. A magenta wall. A green dispensary doorway. A chevron bar painted in six colors. A skyline at f-whatever. A blue LED club at closing. The grade can't be one setting. So we solved every frame on its own. We read white balance only off something neutral on the subject, moved exposure only where a face was under a readability floor, and left the gel rooms exactly as lit.

The light paintings are their own case. Seven frames of the rooftop are long exposures, the light trails from a phone and a lighter drawn in the air. On a long exposure, the trail is the photo. So nothing got lifted. Nothing got denoised. The black stayed black.

## Two versions of every photo

01 NATURAL is the corrected set. Lens, white balance, exposure, color, tone. Then local light in Photoshop on real layers. A soft burn around the subject, a glow on the neon and the bulbs, a slight facial retouch. It's printed with 35 mm color negative grain, strongest in the flat midtones, kept off faces.

02 PULP FICTION is the Tarantino pack look. We picked it for this night with a measured bakeoff of 11 candidates. It holds the blacks under the source with no clipping, where the obvious pick plugged a fifth of the frame to solid black. Warm halation at 12 percent. A newsprint dot screen for the texture, because if a look's gonna be named after that movie, it can be printed on the paper the paperback was.
