The plan was Club 212 on the River Walk. The room was dead, so the night moved, which is the most San Antonio thing a Thursday can do. Up to the 212 rooftop first, where the string lights and the downtown skyline do most of the work, then north to Bar House on North Alamo, then a last dark blue LED club at ten to three in the morning.

212 College Street is worth a line on its own. It is one building with more than one room in it: Mi Vaquita is upstairs, and this house shot MIGX in that room five days earlier. Same address, different night, different floor, completely different photograph. Bar House is the opposite kind of place, a three level bar built into a hundred year old house on North Alamo with a rooftop deck pointed at the skyline, multicolour panelling inside and a DJ on the weekend.

Two cameras went out. A Luna Ultra for the portraits and a wide action camera for the rooftop, which is why a handful of frames are a fisheye of the table with the photographer in them. Both shot RAW, both graded from the RAW.

## What a bar hop asks of a grade

Nothing in a night like this repeats. A rooftop under string lights, a magenta wall, a green dispensary doorway, a chevron bar painted in six colours, a skyline at f-whatever, a blue LED club at closing. The grade cannot be one setting. Every frame was solved on its own: white balance read only off something neutral on the subject, exposure moved only where a face was under a readability floor, and gel rooms left exactly as lit.

The light paintings are their own case. Seven frames of the rooftop are long exposures, the light trails from a phone and a lighter drawn in the air, and on a long exposure the trail is the photograph. Nothing was lifted, nothing was denoised, the black stayed black.

## Two versions of every photograph

01 NATURAL is the corrected set: lens, white balance, exposure, colour, tone, then local light in Photoshop on real layers, a soft burn around the subject, a glow on the neon and the bulbs, a slight facial retouch. Printed with 35 mm colour negative grain, strongest in the flat midtones, kept off faces.

02 PULP FICTION is the Tarantino pack look, picked for this night by a measured bakeoff of eleven candidates: it holds the blacks under the source with no clipping where the obvious pick plugged a fifth of the frame. Warm halation at twelve percent, and a newsprint dot screen for the texture, because if a look is going to be named after that movie it can be printed on the paper the paperback was.
