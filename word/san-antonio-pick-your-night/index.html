San Antonio, Pick Your Night

Friday, November 6 puts Acid Bath at Tech Port, Doja Cat at Frost Bank and Dinosaur Jr. at Paper Tiger. The Current filed two of them under Thursday. Pick one.
0TP-047  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  NOV 04, 2026

Spotted: three rooms on one Friday. Acid Bath, Doja Cat and Dinosaur Jr. all play San Antonio on November 6.

You can't do this week in one night. You can't even do Friday in one night. Pick one.

Friday, November 6

ThePRP, August 10. Acid Bath at the Boeing Center at Tech Port. Support is Twin Tribes, Kylesa and The Atlas Moth. ThePRP calls Acid Bath a resurrected sludge metal group. The page gives no door time and no price.

The presale code was NEWDEATH26. General onsale was Friday, August 14, at 10 a.m. CT. That part is over. The show is not.

Twin Tribes were already on our Fall Run, buried in the Sick New World undercard. Friday they're on a bill in town.

Same night, the arena. Doja Cat with Latto at Frost Bank Center, One Frost Bank Center Drive. The Current lists $62.75 to $408.10, 7:30 p.m. It's her Tour Ma Vie stop, and Frost Bank Center's own calendar has it on November 6.

Same night, St. Mary's. Dinosaur Jr. with Stef Chura at Paper Tiger, 2410 N. St. Mary's St. Doors at 7, show at 8, $39.50 before fees on Paper Tiger's ticket page.

What you read and what's on the record

San Antonio Current, November 3. Its week roundup has headers for Tuesday, Thursday, Sunday and Tuesday. There's no Friday header at all. So Dinosaur Jr. and Doja Cat landed under Thursday, November 5, and Paper Tiger read like two bands in one room at 8 p.m.

But the venues sell it the other way. Paper Tiger has Ceremony on Thursday and Dinosaur Jr. on Friday, same room, back to back. Frost Bank Center has Doja on Friday. The roundup just never printed its Friday.

Thursday, November 5

Ceremony with Slippers and World Peace at Paper Tiger. Doors at 7, show at 8, $22 before fees, all ages. The Current also has JD Simo with Black Joe Lewis that night at the Carlos Alvarez Studio Theater at the Tobin Center, 100 Auditorium Circle, $48 and up, 7:30 p.m.

The one you missed: Tuesday, November 3. The Story So Far at the Aztec Theatre, 104 N. St. Mary's St. $82, 7 p.m. If you're reading this Wednesday, that one was last night.

Sunday and Tuesday

Sunday, November 8. Joe "King" Carrasco with the Hickoids, the Krayolas and Garrett T. Capps at The Lonesome Rose, 2114 N. St. Mary's St. $10, 7 p.m. That's the cheap night.

Tuesday, November 10. Stray Cats at the Majestic Theatre, 224 E. Houston St. $60.39 to $408.75. No time on the page.

So pick your night

Thursday is Ceremony on St. Mary's or JD Simo at the Tobin. Friday is the fight. Acid Bath at Tech Port, Doja at the arena, Dinosaur Jr. at Paper Tiger. Sunday is $10. Tuesday is the Majestic, time unlisted.

One of those. Not all of them.

Boeing Center at Tech Port, Frost Bank Center, Paper Tiger, the Aztec, the Tobin, the Lonesome Rose, the Majestic. November 3 through November 10, 2026.
