THE NIGHT

Four Prints of a Wet Night

WAP in a San Marcos backyard, printed four ways: the corrected night, the flyer's palette, a two-ink riso and a disposable with a date stamp.
0TP-028  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  OCT 02, 2026

WAP was a Friday in a backyard in San Marcos. Private Park, down Nicola Alley by the tracks: doors at eight, music at nine, out at one, ten dollars, eighteen and up, twenty one and up to bring your own, and a DIY slip n slide on the flyer. Hosted by superhellarad and youreyesandearss, with For My Angelite, Blue Navy, Henry J and Cybersonic Sense on the bill, DJ sets between, and sound by CCM Stereo. Cybersonic Sense is GABO, playing the night after the GABO live band's last show at Paper Tiger. House rules on the flyer: no tagging, no littering, no breaking anything.

What the yard has for light is string bulbs in the trees. Everything else is the flash, which caught one guest pushing another around the dirt in a kid's ride-on car, a haircut happening by the string lights with a comb and scissors, a horse mask, a captain's hat and a salute.

Two cards in one night

The camera shot RAW until half past ten and JPEG after that, so the night arrived as two different kinds of file and was graded as two. The RAW frames were developed one by one, each through the gain map the camera writes for that exact frame, on a tone curve fitted to this card, because the usual fitted model failed on this one. The JPEG half went through the same looks from the camera's own files. Fourteen frames shot sideways were turned upright, four zooms between lens steps were cropped back to the framing, and two misses came out. Clipping on the camera's own files was 0.047 percent. On the graded set it is zero.

Four versions of every photograph

01 CINEMATIC is the corrected night.

02 WAP is the flyer's palette, pushed.

03 RISO 2-INK prints every frame as if it came off a risograph in two inks, pink and blue, overprinting into violet where they cross. It is the grainiest of the four on purpose, and its whites are the paper.

04 FUNSAVER is a disposable camera: a vignette and the orange seven segment date stamp in the corner, '26 9 25, the way the drugstore used to print it.

All four carry a real texture made in Photoshop, scans laid over each frame as live High Pass layers so the grain sits in the picture and not on top of it. The first pass ran too heavy and put canvas weave on skin when you zoomed in. It was pulled back by half, faces held to a fifth of the strength and skin to about a third, and measured again on the files Photoshop actually wrote, because a High Pass in Soft Light lands harder in Photoshop than it does in a test render.

The live photos

One frame in the gallery is not a photo that was taken. It was pulled out of a live photo, the three second clip the camera records around each shot, one of twelve moments picked by eye from those clips and graded as photographs of their own. Under the string lights the clips pulse, so the bright frames were found first; a sharpness score on the dim ones reads noise as detail.

Where it lives

The cleared frames from WAP, all four versions of each, full quality, free to download. Four independent reviewers read every candidate first, same as every night here, and at a swimsuit party in a backyard they read it slowly.
