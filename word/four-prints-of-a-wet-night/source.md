WAP was a Friday in a backyard in San Marcos. Private Park, down Nicola Alley by the tracks. Doors at eight, music at nine, out at one, ten dollars, 18 and up, 21 and up to bring your own. Plus a DIY slip n slide on the flyer. The hosts were superhellarad and youreyesandearss. For My Angelite, Blue Navy, Henry J and Cybersonic Sense were on the bill. DJ sets between, sound by CCM Stereo. Cybersonic Sense is GABO, playing the night after the GABO live band's last show at Paper Tiger. House rules on the flyer. No tagging, no littering, no breaking anything.

For light, the yard has string bulbs in the trees. Everything else is the flash. It caught one guest pushing another around the dirt in a kid's ride-on car. A haircut happening by the string lights with a comb and scissors. A horse mask. A captain's hat and a salute.

## Two cards in one night

The camera shot RAW until half past ten and JPEG after that. So the night came in as two different kinds of file, and we graded it as two. We developed the RAW frames one by one, each through the gain map the camera writes for that exact frame. The usual fitted model failed on this card, so we fitted a tone curve to this one. The JPEG half went through the same looks, starting from the camera's own files.

14 frames were shot sideways, so we turned them upright. We cropped four zooms between lens steps back to the framing, and two misses came out. Clipping on the camera's own files was 0.047 percent. On the graded set it's zero.

## Four versions of every photo

01 CINEMATIC is the corrected night.

02 WAP is the flyer's palette, pushed.

03 RISO 2-INK prints every frame like it came off a risograph in two inks. Pink and blue, overprinting into violet where they cross. It's the grainiest of the four on purpose, and its whites are the paper.

04 FUNSAVER is a disposable camera. You get a vignette and the orange seven segment date stamp in the corner, '26 9 25, the way the drugstore used to print it.

All four carry a real texture we made in Photoshop. We laid scans over each frame as live High Pass layers, so the grain sits in the picture and not on top of it. The first pass ran too heavy. Zoom in and there was canvas weave on skin. So we pulled it back by half, and held faces to a fifth of the strength and skin to about a third. Then we measured again on the files Photoshop actually wrote, because a High Pass in Soft Light lands harder in Photoshop than it does in a test render.

## The live photos

One frame in the gallery isn't a photo that was taken. We pulled it out of a live photo, the three second clip the camera records around each shot. It's one of 12 moments we picked by eye from those clips and graded as photos of their own. Under the string lights the clips pulse. So we found the bright frames first, because a sharpness score on the dim ones reads noise as detail.

## Where it lives

[The cleared frames from WAP](https://0fftheprint.com/events/2026-09-25-wap/) are up, all four versions of each, full quality, free to download. Four independent reviewers read every candidate first, same as every night here. At a swimsuit party in a backyard, they read it slowly.
