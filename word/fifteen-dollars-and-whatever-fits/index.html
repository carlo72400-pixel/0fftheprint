Fifteen Dollars And Whatever Fits

Thrift @ The Warehouse fills a bag for fifteen dollars every third Saturday. We hit both Austin stores before noon. Here is what came out of the pile.
0TP-039  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  OCT 18, 2026
Spotted: a Dragon Ball Z tee held up to the sun on South Congress, 10:21 on a Saturday morning. Every panel of the saga on one shirt.

You think the good stuff's gone by noon. Not on the third Saturday.

That's Fill-a-Bag at Thrift @ The Warehouse. It's every third Saturday, 10 to 4. One bag, $15, whatever fits in it. Community Impact put the rule in print in March. So we went to see if it holds.

So we hit both stores back to back

The chain started in San Antonio. It came up to Austin last December on West 34th Street, then opened a second store on South Congress. Same sale, two piles, one river between them.

South Congress from 10:13 to 10:50. 34th Street from 11:27 to 12:19. Two cameras at the tables and a 360 on a stick, pointed straight down into the pile.

Count the finds

Plaid shorts. Gap jeans. A Vans jacket. A studded jean vest with a lace back. A Carhartt long sleeve in maroon. A red leather jacket. A pink polo. An Aeropostale shirt. Betty Boop on orange, printed I AM the Treat. A black tee that reads THIS IS GOING TO RUIN THE TOUR.

One shopper filled three bags. $15 a bag. Nobody said one.

But watch it before you go

The morning turned into three edits. PAIN cuts it to PinkPantheress in 30 seconds.

WELCOME BACK is 58 seconds of what's in your bag. Somebody turns up at both stores.

ITEM CALL names 15 finds in 28 seconds on a Carti instrumental. One name per hit.

What you heard and what's on the record

You heard 34th Street throws a night market. Community Impact reported it in March as every fourth Friday, 6 to 11, with live music, DJs and vendors. The fourth Friday this month is October 23. Nobody's told us what this one looks like yet. Check the store's page before you drive.

So mark your own calendar

Austin's about an hour and 20 minutes up I-35 from San Antonio. The next third Saturday is November 21. Doors open at 10. Be there when they do.

The cleared frames from Fill-a-Bag are up, both prints of every one, full quality, free to download. Four independent reviewers read every candidate first, same as every night here.
