> Spotted: a Dragon Ball Z tee held up to the sun on South Congress at 10:21 on a Saturday morning. Every panel of the saga on one shirt.

You think the good stuff is gone by noon. Not on the third Saturday.

That is Fill-a-Bag at Thrift @ The Warehouse. Every third Saturday, 10 to 4. One bag, fifteen dollars, whatever fits in it. Community Impact put the rule in print in March. So we went to see if it holds.

## So we did both stores before noon

The chain started in San Antonio. It came up to Austin last December on West 34th Street, then opened a second store on South Congress. Same sale, two piles, one river between them.

South Congress from 10:13 to 10:50. 34th Street from 11:27 to 12:19. Two cameras at the tables and a 360 on a stick, pointed straight down into the pile.

## Count the finds

Plaid shorts. Gap jeans. A Vans jacket. A studded jean vest with a lace back. A Carhartt long sleeve in maroon. A red leather jacket. A pink polo. An Aeropostale shirt. Betty Boop on orange, printed I AM the Treat. A black tee that reads THIS IS GOING TO RUIN THE TOUR.

One shopper filled three bags. Fifteen dollars a bag. Nobody said one.

## What you heard and what is on the record

You heard 34th Street throws a night market. Community Impact reported it in March: every fourth Friday, 6 to 11, live music, DJs and vendors. The fourth Friday this month is 23 October. Nobody has told us what this one looks like yet. Check the store's page before you drive.

## So mark your own calendar

Austin is about an hour and twenty minutes up I-35 from San Antonio. The next third Saturday is 21 November. The doors open at 10. Be there when they do.
