THE FESTIVAL

Half A Reign In Blood

Sick New World's first Texas day put about 80,000 people at a racetrack. AFI ran the afternoon. Slayer played half of Reign in Blood. We said front to back.
0TP-044  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  OCT 27, 2026

Slayer's fire at Wacken Open Air, Germany, August 2019, by Sven Mandel, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0. Cropped.

Spotted: A wall of fire across Slayer's stage during "Raining Blood" at Texas Motor Speedway. The screens rained blood too.

About 80,000 people were there, the Dallas Observer says. And we owe you a correction. It's further down.

Here's the day

Saturday, October 24. Sick New World's first time outside Las Vegas. Kerrang counted 49 bands in one day, at a sold-out racetrack in Fort Worth.

It was hot. Security at the stage fronts threw water into the crowd. The Observer saw fans crowd-surfed forward with heat exhaustion and handed to medics. Food lines ran so long that waiting meant missing bands. Plenty of people grabbed whatever was fastest. A lot of fans in their 40s and 50s sat in the speedway stands and watched the big screen. That's not giving up. That's knowing your knees.

The sets people are still talking about

AFI got the 4 o'clock slot and no pyro. 10 songs, "Girl's Not Grey" to "Miss Murder," and the Observer said it may have been the peak of the festival. Earlier, Alien Ant Farm asked the crowd who else they came for. Dryden Mitchell answered for himself. AFI, "to see if that guy's real."

Boy Harsher played Tannahill's in Fort Worth the night before. Then Jae Matthews told a metal crowd it was allowed to dance, and people did, while Slayer's fire went off across the grounds.

Kerrang's pick of the daytime sets was Knocked Loose. Deftones played 18 songs and saved "Change (In the House of Flies)" for fourth from the end.

System of a Down closed with nobody playing against them. Daron Malakian told the crowd none of this happens without Slayer. He said he still had a Slayer poster on his wall when Slayer took them on their first tour. They ended on "Sugar."

What you heard and what's on the record

You heard Slayer was playing Reign in Blood front to back. You heard it from us. Our Fall Run said it in August.

But Blabbermouth had it back on April 23. Only three U.S. headline dates get the full album. Shakopee, Minnesota on September 4, and two nights at the Kia Forum. Festival sets would be different. So Texas got 13 songs, per setlist.fm, and 5 were from Reign in Blood. "Angel of Death," "Altar of Sacrifice," "Jesus Saves," "Postmortem" and "Raining Blood." Half the album, and it still closed on fire. That one's on us.

You also heard people went down all day. The Observer saw the heat handoffs. Lambgoat and The Mosh say attendees posted about medical emergencies and bad cell signal. Both say no deaths or serious injuries have been confirmed. Neither had a word from the festival.

So mark your own calendar

Want the whole album? November 13 and 14 at the Kia Forum in Inglewood. The speedway was a 287-mile drive from downtown San Antonio. The Forum's a flight.

Presale for this one opened October 24, 2025. One year to the day. As of Tuesday the official page just says thank you, Texas. No 2027 date. The Observer asked the organizers to keep it here and bring more tacos. We second the tacos.
