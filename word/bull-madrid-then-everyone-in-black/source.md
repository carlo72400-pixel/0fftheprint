Two rooms. One Saturday. They couldn't have been less alike.

## The ring

First up was the backyard of El Luchador Bar on Roosevelt. It's a lucha libre bar south of Southtown, and a few nights a year it turns the patio into a live wrestling venue. There's a ring under string lights and floodlights, a chain-link fence around it, a bar-back wall of beer signs behind the far ropes and a standing crowd with their phones up. No gels, no flash, just tungsten and whatever the floods threw.

Bull Madrid in the black and gold singlet, against a red-masked luchador with Chido written across his shorts. A referee. An announcer with a corded microphone, and a bearded guy in a vest who took it off him. At one point somebody pulled out an accordion. At a wrestling match.

Two matches, 50 minutes, 15 stills and half an hour of video. We graded all of it three ways. The first is a look built for the ring itself, tungsten gold with the red masks pushed. Reservoir Dogs is the truest color of the three. Death Proof is a grindhouse print with the greens muted and a hair of fade.

## Then downtown

By 11 the camera was upstairs at Club 212 on the River Walk, the room with the biggest LED wall in the city. The wall said MIGX. Everyone in black. Red neon on one side. Blue and magenta LED on the other. Hookah on the tables, and bottle-service boards in cow print that read BADDIES POP BOTTLES and DEMON TIME. We shot 233 frames between 11 and a quarter to two. Almost all of them are flash portraits of people who came dressed for exactly this.

DEMON TIME is the custom grade, named off the club's own sign. Magenta and red louder, cool shadows, hot flash whites. Tenet's the cool clean one. Pulp Fiction's the warm one with the friendliest skin. Plus two creative prints we built in Photoshop as real layers. HELL THERMAL is a solarized thermal ramp that runs black to blood to hot pink to white. BLACKLIGHT UV is a violet room where the whites and the skin glow. Open any frame and tap between all five.

## Where it lives

Both nights are up as their own galleries, [the ring](https://0fftheprint.com/events/2026-09-12-lucha-night/) and [the club](https://0fftheprint.com/events/2026-09-12-migx/). Full quality, free to download, with the clips graded to match. The ring gets three grades. The club gets five. If you were in either room, you're probably in one of them.
