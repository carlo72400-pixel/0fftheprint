Two rooms, one Saturday, and they could not have been less alike.

## The ring

The first was the backyard of El Luchador Bar on Roosevelt, a lucha libre bar south of Southtown that turns its patio into a live wrestling venue a few nights a year: a ring under string lights and floodlights, a chain-link fence around it, a bar-back wall of beer signs behind the far ropes, and a standing crowd with their phones up. No gels, no flash, just tungsten and whatever the floods threw. Bull Madrid, in the black and gold singlet, against a red-masked luchador with Chido written across his shorts. A referee. An announcer with a corded microphone and a bearded man in a vest who took it off him. At one point somebody produced an accordion, which is the most San Antonio thing that has ever happened at a wrestling match.

Two matches, fifty minutes, fifteen stills and half an hour of video, all of it graded three ways: a look built for the ring itself, tungsten gold with the red masks pushed; Reservoir Dogs, which is the truest colour of the three; and Death Proof, a grindhouse print with the greens muted and a hair of fade.

## Then downtown

By eleven the camera was on the River Walk at Mi Vaquita, upstairs, the room with the biggest LED wall in the city, and the wall said MIGX. Everyone in black. Red neon on one side, blue and magenta LED on the other, hookah on the tables, bottle-service boards in cow print that read BADDIES POP BOTTLES and DEMON TIME. Two hundred and thirty three frames between eleven and a quarter to two, almost all of them flash portraits of people who came dressed for exactly this.

DEMON TIME is the custom grade, named off the club's own sign: magenta and red louder, cool shadows, hot flash whites. Tenet is the cool clean one. Pulp Fiction is the warm one with the friendliest skin. And then two creative prints built in Photoshop as real layers: HELL THERMAL, a solarised thermal ramp that runs black to blood to hot pink to white, and BLACKLIGHT UV, a violet room where the whites and the skin glow. Open any frame and tap between all five.

## Where it lives

Both nights are up as their own galleries, [the ring](https://0fftheprint.com/events/2026-09-12-lucha-night/) and [the club](https://0fftheprint.com/events/2026-09-12-migx/), full quality, free to download, with the clips graded to match. The ring gets three grades, the club gets five. If you were in either room you are probably in one of them.

San Antonio, Saturday 12 September 2026. El Luchador at 8:48, Mi Vaquita at 10:54, home at two.
