ComplexCon turns ten this year. The label headlining it is the one that spent five years acting like it didn't want to be looked at.

Playboi Carti and Ken Carson top the bill in Los Angeles on October 3 and 4. Destroy Lonely's on it too. That's not a guest spot. That's Opium taking the anniversary.

## Sit with this part

Opium was built on scarcity. No interviews for years. No tracklists until the thing was already out. Album rollouts that played more like leaks than campaigns. And a visual language that made the whole roster look like one person in different masks. It was never supposed to work at scale. Scarcity doesn't usually survive contact with a convention floor.

But it survived anyway, and the last two months are the proof. Ken Carson put out his fifth album in July. He's on the road behind it. He also did a 14 piece capsule with WWE and Fanatics that went live at the end of the month. Nobody would've believed that sentence in 2022. It's Attitude Era wrestlers like Stone Cold and the Undertaker, plus Rhea Ripley from right now, all drawn in his own graphic language, with the album title on a bandana.

The joke is that none of it reads as a sellout move, because the wrestling thing was never a bit. He's been a wrestling head the whole time. The merch is just the first time the rest of us got to see it.

## What's still owed

The Ken Carson and Destroy Lonely joint project's been teased for most of the year. But it still has no title and no date. Lonely has said he's focused on finishing it. That's the last piece of the run.

## If you're in Texas

The tour hits Austin on September 15 at the Moody Center, then Irving the night after. Both are already on our calendar, and the Austin bill's the more interesting of the two.
