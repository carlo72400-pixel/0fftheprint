THE TAKEOVER

Opium Bought The Whole Convention

ComplexCon turns ten and the label built on never being looked at is headlining it. Ken Carson is playing Austin on September 15.
0TP-010  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  AUG 27, 2026

Ken Carson, WTF Tour, Düsseldorf 2026, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0. Cropped.

ComplexCon turns ten this year, and the label headlining it is the one that spent five years acting like it did not want to be looked at.

Playboi Carti and Ken Carson top the bill in Los Angeles on October 3 and 4. Destroy Lonely is on it too. That is not a guest spot, that is Opium taking the anniversary.

The part worth sitting with

Opium was built on scarcity. No interviews for years, no tracklists until the thing was already out, album rollouts that behaved more like leaks than campaigns, and a visual language that made the whole roster look like one person in different masks. It was never supposed to work at scale. Scarcity does not usually survive contact with a convention floor.

It survived anyway, and the last two months are the proof. Ken Carson put out his fifth album in July. He is on the road behind it. He also did a fourteen piece capsule with WWE and Fanatics that went live at the end of the month, which is a sentence nobody would have believed in 2022: Attitude Era wrestlers, Stone Cold and the Undertaker and Rhea Ripley, drawn in his own graphic language, with the album title on a bandana.

The joke is that none of it reads as a sellout move, because the wrestling thing was never a bit. He has been a wrestling head the whole time. The merch is just the first time the rest of us got to see it.

What is still owed

The Ken Carson and Destroy Lonely joint project has been teased for most of the year and still has no title and no date. Lonely has said he is focused on finishing it. That is the last piece of the run.

If you are in Texas

The tour hits Austin on September 15 at the Moody Center, and Irving the night after. Both are on our calendar already, and the Austin bill is the more interesting of the two.
