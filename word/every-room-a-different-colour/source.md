New Guild Co-op is a housing co-op in Austin. So the venue was somebody's house. Not a house with a stage in it. A house. The living rooms were lit by whatever lamps live there. The porch was magenta because somebody put a magenta bulb in it. The back room had a green wash and a DJ table, and the alley out back was graffiti and flash and nothing else.

The night was called BRAINROT, and it was a fundraiser before it was a party. The money went to a friend's cancer treatment. The flyer was a thermal false-color image with chrome WordArt on top. It looks like a joke until you realize it's doing exactly what a flyer's supposed to do.

## The day before the night

The camera went out at two in the afternoon. First stop was the HOPE Outdoor Gallery, the graffiti park, where a handful of people in Clown Underground shirts stood against the walls in clown masks and let the paint do the talking. Then dusk at the venue. The string lights came on one by one and the porch went pink.

## Every room a different problem

Here's what a house show gives you that a club never does. A club has one lighting rig and one color. Solve it once and you've solved the night. A house has six rooms and six answers. Tungsten in the living room. Blue LED in the front room. Green on the dance floor. Magenta on the porch. Flash in the alley. People move between them all night, so the same face goes orange, then blue, then green, then pink, all inside an hour.

So we graded it twice. Once as a party, warm and loud, and the room got to be as pink as it really was. Once as BRAINROT, with a false-color ramp lifted straight off the night's own flyer, so the photos match the poster that got people in the door.
