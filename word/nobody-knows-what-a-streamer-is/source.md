You know him from YouTube, videos like The Highschool Bigger Than Most Colleges. Now he's up against the guy who won the last two shows.

## Here's the ballot

Streamer of the Year is Joe Bartolozzi, CaseOh, IShowSpeed, Stable Ronaldo and Jynxzi. Joe's also up for President of Chat, which used to be called Best Just Chatting, and for Best Breakout Streamer. BruceDropEmOff's in President of Chat too. PlaqueBoyMax is up for Best Music Streamer.

## But the VTuber race is a rerun

Best VTuber is Ironmouse, TheBurntPeanut, Gigi Murin, Koseki Bijou and Crelly. Ironmouse won it three years running. Then last December the Peanut took it. Dexerto calls him a self-described non-VTuber, and his avatar is an animated peanut with human eyes and a mouth. He's also up for Gamer of the Year and Best FPS Streamer. So a peanut is in three categories, and one of them is the one he says he isn't.

## What you heard and what's on the record

You heard the Peanut changed his model to calm everybody down. He didn't. On his October 17 stream he showed an edited peanut with long orange hair and pitched it as unity. Dexerto ran the clip. It was a bit.

## So what's a streamer now

A YouTuber with a Twitch channel. A peanut that says he isn't a VTuber. A defending champ whose World Cup tour is up for Best Streamed Series. We think that's the point. A streamer isn't a camera and a chair anymore. It's whoever you keep the tab open for.
