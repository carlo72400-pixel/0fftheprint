> Spotted: a liability waiver, one page, on a movie's website.

We have not seen anybody else write about it. So here.

Start with what you already know. On the weekend of 18 September a teaser ran in theatres ahead of Resident Evil. It looked like security camera footage from inside a Halloween haunt. That Monday A24 put a 42 second cut online. A24 invites you, it says. This fall. Then a title, Slayground, and an address.

## Go to the address

The address is [runlikehell.com](https://www.runlikehell.com/) and there is a clock on it. We read the site's own code. The clock runs out on Tuesday 20 October at 9 in the morning Eastern, which is 8 o'clock in San Antonio. If you read Monday the 19th somewhere, the code says Tuesday.

On 1 October the posters came, and they stopped pretending to be for a movie. They call it a haunted house with no exit and they tell you to run like hell. No date. No credits.

And at the bottom of the site, down with the privacy policy and the terms, there is now a link that says Waiver.

## Read the waiver

One page. The file says it was made on 30 September. It reads like the form a real haunt hands you at the door: fear, fainting, seizure, heart attack, all of it yours to assume. It tells you to enter at your own risk.

And it says Slayground operates under Oregon law. Oregon is where Saulnier set Green Room, in the woods outside Portland. A24 put that one out too.

Is it a prop, or is it paperwork for a door you can actually walk through? A24 has not said.

## But here is what nobody has confirmed

The movie is real. Deadline announced it in June 2025 under the title October, written and directed by Jeremy Saulnier, who made Green Room and Rebel Ridge. Cory Michael Smith leads. Imogen Poots, Matty Matheson, Stephen Root, Young Mazino and James Badge Dale are in it.

Everything past that is noise until A24 talks. There is no release date. The teaser says this fall and stops. There is no official plot. And the Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross score you keep hearing about traces back to one line on World of Reel, where the writer said he had heard it.

So nobody outside the building knows what happens when the clock hits zero. A trailer, a date, or a door.

## Why we are watching

Saulnier brought Green Room to Fantastic Fest in Austin in 2015. The photograph at the top of this page is from that week. On Sunday Beyond Fest had him booked in Los Angeles for the film's tenth anniversary. If he said one word about Slayground from that stage, we have not seen it in print.

Eleven years after Austin he is selling a movie as a place you can walk into, in October, in a state where people line up to be chased through a warehouse.

So set an alarm.
