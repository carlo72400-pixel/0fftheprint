Horror's Farm League Is The Internet

A 14-minute YouTube short with Pauly Shore passed 5.5 million views in 9 days. A Reddit post just got a movie deal. Studios don't find horror now. They scroll.
0TP-041  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  OCT 21, 2026

An empty office, September 2011, by Ged Carroll, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0. Cropped.

Spotted: a 14-minute horror short with Pauly Shore in it, past 5.5 million views on YouTube in 9 days.

Studios don't find horror anymore. They scroll for it.

Here's the short

It's called Remain Calm. Jake Lewis wrote and directed it. Pauly Shore and Nick Swardson are in it, and Doug Jones plays the Grin Man. The monster, Mr. Frett, can only see you when you're anxious. So the whole movie is people trying to stay calm, which is kinda the hardest thing you can ask a person to do in 2026.

Every effect is practical. Bloody Disgusting counted 5.5 million views since October 12 and said it wouldn't be surprised if it turned into a feature.

Then a Reddit post got a movie deal

On October 20, the Deadstream directors, Joseph and Vanessa Winter, signed on to make Future Me. It started on r/nosleep, the subreddit where people post horror they wrote themselves. Daria Vasileva wrote it. A guy gets an email from his past self through the FutureMe site, and it goes bad from there.

The producer's statement says it straight. After Backrooms, internet lore is fair game for movies.

But this isn't new

Slender Man started as a Photoshop contest entry on the Something Awful forums on June 10, 2009. Eric Knudsen made him. On October 20, 2026, he was a Fortnite skin.

Skinamarink came off a YouTube channel called Bitesized Nightmares. It cost $15,000 and made about $2 million at the box office. Markiplier put out Iron Lung himself on January 30. It opened to $18.2 million.

And Backrooms. Kane Parsons posted the first video January 7, 2022. A24 put the movie out May 29. It opened to $81.4 million, the biggest opening A24 has ever had, and it's past $400 million worldwide. We wrote about him in Sixteen, A Laptop, Four Hundred Million.

So that's the farm league

You make it cheap. You post it. A million people find it before any studio does. So the studio calls. We think that's the whole pipeline now.

What you heard and what's on the record

You heard the Slender Man skin was free. Not for everybody. It came out of the Slender Man Cup, and you had to place high enough to get it. Beebom guessed it might hit the shop later for 1,500 V-Bucks. Nobody's confirmed that.

So watch it tonight

Remain Calm is free on YouTube, 14 minutes, on The Lewis Brothers' channel. Deadstream premiered at SXSW in Austin in 2022, and Shudder bought it before it premiered. So the next one might show up an hour and 20 minutes up I-35.
