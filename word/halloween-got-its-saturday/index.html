Halloween Got Its Saturday

Halloween's on a Saturday for the first time since 2020, and 2020 didn't count. The next one's 2037. Here's what people are wearing and which night to pick.

But you're not here for Paris. Halloween's on a Saturday this year.

Check the calendar

The last Saturday one was 2020, and the CDC called door-to-door trick-or-treating high risk that year, so that one doesn't count.

Before that it was 2015. After this it's 2037. If you just turned 21, you were 10 the last time it counted.

Here's what people are wearing

The National Retail Federation says America's spending a record $13.5 billion on Halloween this year, up from $13.1 billion. Costumes are the biggest slice at $4.4 billion. Candy's $4.1 billion.

It asked 7,889 people in September. Witch is the top adult costume with 4.8 million and vampire's right behind it at 4.2 million, so Batman's a distant third at 2 million. Kids are going Spider-Man, 4.6 million of them, and pets are going pumpkin.

Google's list looks nothing like that. Variety ran it October 14. Number 1 is Buddy, the orange unicorn from Casper Kelly's horror movie Buddy. Keegan-Michael Key plays him. Kelly's the Too Many Cooks guy.

NeeDoh, the squishy toy, is number 2. Oliver Tree is number 6. The person.

What you heard and what's on the record

You heard everybody's going as the unicorn. Not quite. Google's list is the searches that spiked the most, and a spike isn't a crowd. The NRF counted people. Witches won.

You also heard somebody tried to move Halloween to a Saturday for good. That one's real. In 2018 the Halloween & Costume Association, a trade group for costume and party companies, started a Change.org petition to move it to the last Saturday of October. CBS News counted more than 80,000 signatures. CBS also pointed out it's not a federal holiday, so the president couldn't move it anyway.

So nothing moved. But this year the last Saturday of October is the 31st. The calendar did it for free.

So pick your night

As of Thursday, AccuWeather gives San Antonio an 88 percent chance of rain Friday night. Saturday night it's 2 percent. Saturday afternoon's still 55 percent, so get dressed late.

In Austin on Saturday, Madeon plays ACL Live at 8 with Anamanaguchi opening. The Jesus and Mary Chain and Protomartyr play KUTX's Halloween show at the Far Out, and there's a costume contest. Austin's about 80 miles up I-35.

Megan Thee Stallion's Hottieween is Friday at Zouk in Las Vegas, with Cirque du Soleil, per Complex. That's a flight. Pack the costume flat.

The rest of the weekend's on the Crypt, San Antonio and Austin, Friday and Saturday both.

Saturday, October 31, 2026. Next year it's a Sunday.
