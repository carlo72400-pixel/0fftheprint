THE RUN

Both Bands Left Town

Blue Navy put out 6 songs and landed on a college radio flyer. Street Lamp booked 7 Texas shows in 11 days. We shot both in September. Here's where they go.
0TP-052  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  NOV 09, 2026
Spotted: Blue Navy at the bottom of a college radio flyer. Last set of the night. 12:15.

You saw them in a backyard. So did we.

Here's what dropped

Blue Navy played WAP on September 25. That was a Friday in a yard in San Marcos, and we shot it. On Tuesday, November 3, the band posted a few plain lines. Their record is out. It's 6 songs. More is coming soon.

No rollout. No countdown. Just a number.

But then the radio stations showed up

KTSW is the student station at Texas State. KVRX is the student station at UT Austin. On November 6 KTSW posted that the two are throwing one Battle of the Bands, and that it took years to set up. Blue Navy's own post bills it Austin vs San Marcos.

It's Saturday, November 14, at Pearl Street Co-op in Austin. Five bands. SPELL-X6 at 9. In The Shade at 10. never odd or even at 10:45. Leche at 11:30. Blue Navy at 12:15.

So the band from the backyard closes the night.

So here's the other band

Street Lamp opened HARAMBE 4 EVER at Paper Tiger on September 24. First of four, 8 o'clock, one red light the whole night. On November 8 they posted a run they're calling Texas Tango. That's 7 shows in 11 days.

November 13, Dallas. November 14, San Antonio, in Legacy Park with The Down Market. November 15, Austin, at Hotel Vegas. November 20, McAllen, at The Flying Walrus. November 21, Corpus Christi. November 22, Houston. November 23, back in San Antonio at The Mix.

The Houston post says it's their first time back in way more than a year. It's $5 and all ages. McAllen is $10, doors at 8.

What you heard and what's on the record

It'd be easy to say both bands blew up. That's not on the record. What's on the record is 6 songs, 7 dates and one flyer with two radio stations on it. Nobody's posted how the battle gets judged. Nobody's said who's playing for which city.

But in September these two were playing a yard and a side stage. Now one's on the road and the other's in a radio fight.

So mark your own calendar

Saturday looks like a problem. Street Lamp is in San Antonio and Blue Navy is 80 miles north in Austin, on the same day. But The Down Market's thing at Legacy Park runs 6:30 to 9, and Blue Navy isn't on till 12:15. So you can do both.

The Austin show on the 15th is 21 and up. If you're in San Antonio and you miss the 14th, The Mix on the 23rd is the makeup date.

The cleared frames from WAP and the ones from HARAMBE 4 EVER are still up.

San Marcos and San Antonio. Monday, November 9, 2026.
