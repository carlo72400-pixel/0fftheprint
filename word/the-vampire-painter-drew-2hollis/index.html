The Vampire Painter Drew 2hollis

Yoshitaka Amano has drawn Vampire Hunter D since 1983. Now he has drawn the cover of Pirouette, out November 11. His own paintings are hanging in Houston.
0TP-032  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  OCT 04, 2026

Yoshitaka Amano at Toulouse Game Show 2012, by N'gobo, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0. Cropped.

Spotted: the man who drew Vampire Hunter D, drawing 2hollis.

You did not have that one on your card. Neither did we.

Here is what is on the record. On Tuesday 22 September 2hollis announced his fifth album. It is called Pirouette and it is out on 11 November on Interscope. That makes five albums in five years, so the announcement was not the news.

The cover was the news.

The hand that drew it

Yoshitaka Amano is 74. He has illustrated the Vampire Hunter D novels since 1983. He has drawn for Final Fantasy since 1987, the logos and the art behind them. Pale faces, long coats, hair that moves like smoke. If you grew up near a PlayStation or an anime shelf you know the line before you know the name.

The label calls the Pirouette cover hand drawn. Amano's own site says the same.

Then the painter talked. In the label's announcement he said 2hollis felt close to the kind of character he has always drawn, "as though he had stepped directly out of one of my paintings."

So a man who has spent 43 years drawing a vampire hunter looked at a 22 year old and saw one of his own. We did not say it. He did.

But do not repeat what you heard

You heard Playboi Carti and Ken Carson are on the album. Nobody has announced a single feature. That line about collaborations comes out of the label's bio, and it was already sitting there in July. Apple's pre-add page shows sixteen slots and three titles. Hurt, Sex and Warhorse. Every one is credited to him alone.

You heard there is a tour. There is not. The only dates we could find are a festival in San Diego on 10 October and Camp Flog Gnaw in Los Angeles on 14 November. Nothing in Texas.

So here is your Texas date

2hollis is not coming. The painter is already here.

Yoshitaka Amano: Big Bang opened on 23 September at Asia Society Texas Center in Houston. About forty works in oil, ink and automotive paint. Close to thirty years of the painting he does away from the games. One of them runs over fifty feet. It is up until 7 February.

Eight dollars at the door. Free on Thursdays. Free with a student ID. Closed Monday and Tuesday.

That is three hours down I-10, so the order is simple. See the paintings first. Hear the record on the 11th. Then look at that cover again and tell us he was wrong.

Wednesday 11 November 2026. Pirouette, Interscope. Until then: Asia Society Texas Center, 1370 Southmore Blvd, Houston.
