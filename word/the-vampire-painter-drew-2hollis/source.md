> Spotted: the guy who drew Vampire Hunter D, drawing 2hollis.

You didn't have that one on your card. Neither did we.

Here's what's on the record. 2hollis announced his fifth album on Tuesday, September 22. It's called Pirouette, and it's out November 11 on Interscope. That makes five albums in five years, so the announcement wasn't the news.

The cover was the news.

## The hand that drew it

Yoshitaka Amano is 74. He's illustrated the Vampire Hunter D novels since 1983. He's drawn for Final Fantasy since 1987, the logos and the art behind them. Pale faces, long coats, hair that moves like smoke. If you grew up near a PlayStation or an anime shelf, you know the line before you know the name.

The label calls the Pirouette cover hand drawn, and Amano's own site says the same.

Then the painter talked. In the label's announcement, he said 2hollis felt close to the kind of character he's always drawn, "as though he had stepped directly out of one of my paintings."

So a guy who's spent 43 years drawing a vampire hunter looked at a 22 year old and saw one of his own. We didn't say it. He did.

## But don't repeat what you heard

You heard Playboi Carti and Ken Carson are on the album. Nobody's announced a single feature. That line about collaborations comes out of the label's bio, and it was already sitting there in July. Apple's pre-add page shows 16 slots and three titles. Hurt, Sex and Warhorse. Every one is credited to him alone.

You heard there's a tour. There is not. The only dates we could find are a festival in San Diego on October 10 and Camp Flog Gnaw in Los Angeles on November 14. Nothing in Texas.
