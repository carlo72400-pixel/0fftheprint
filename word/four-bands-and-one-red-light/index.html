THE NIGHT

Four Bands and One Red Light

HARAMBE 4 EVER on the Paper Tiger side stage: three cameras, one red wash, and the skin brought back on the faces only.
0TP-027  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  OCT 02, 2026

HARAMBE 4 EVER was a Thursday on the side stage at Paper Tiger. Four bands, on a flyer of sunset clouds and heaven light. Streetlamp at 8, Midnight Dawn at 9, Black Nail Polish at 10 and GABO at 11, the last show from GABO's live band before a break. Between sets the patio did what a Paper Tiger patio does, which is pose for anybody holding a camera. After midnight the back room turned into a DJ set in a fog of haze.

The side stage is lit by one color. A near monochrome red wash, all night, for every band. On the action camera it doesn't even read as red. That sensor turns it hot pink.

Three cameras. Three problems.

A Luna Ultra on the edge of the stage, shooting RAW and live photos. An Ace Pro 2 in the pit, also RAW. An X6 planted for 360 video. We pulled stills from it.

Each one broke something different. The Ace saves two kinds of JPEG. One's a full fisheye, the other's a straightened crop of the middle. It also writes no orientation at all. So we turned 67 frames by hand, and we rendered every one of them from the RAW through the lens model the factory stores inside the file, back to the framing that was actually shot.

The X6 records the whole sphere at 3840 by 1920. That leaves a normal view of the stage about 960 pixels wide. So those stills are honest about their resolution. We picked them for the moment, not the sharpness.

But the video stills came out milky at first. The camera's video is limited range, and it got decoded as full range, which lifts every black to gray. Quality control caught it. We fixed the floor and rendered all the video stills again.

Skin back. Room stays red.

Stage light is final. That's the rule here. A red room's supposed to look like a red room. But this night came with one exception, asked for out loud. Bring the skin back on the faces. So the faces got color from the night's own skin. We measured it off people standing on the patio and under the bar's white light, outside the gel. Then we moved it onto the faces of the people on stage, before any look went on. Only the faces. The room, the haze, the amps and the crowd stay exactly as red as they were.

Two versions of every photo

01 SIDE STAGE is a restrained concert grade built for this room. We ran every cube in the library on these frames first. All 59 of them. None of them beat the one we made here.

02 HEAVEN LIGHT takes the flyer at its word. It turns the red wash into warm gold, so it reads like a sunset instead of a siren. The lights get a god-ray glow at a quarter strength. But it runs copper on the reddest frames. So SIDE STAGE leads the gallery.

We finished both in Photoshop with a grain pass solved to a measured target per camera. A phone sensor, an action camera and a 360 rig don't carry the same noise. So they shouldn't end up wearing the same grain.

Where it lives

The cleared frames from HARAMBE 4 EVER are up, both versions of each, full quality, free to download. Four independent reviewers read every candidate first, same as every night here.
