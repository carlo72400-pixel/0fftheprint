Young Thug's New Generation Tour comes through Texas at the end of the month, and San Antonio isn't on it. Austin gets him Wednesday, September 30 at the Moody Amphitheater at Waterloo Park, doors at 6:30, show at 7. Nav's the special guest, with the YSL bench underneath. That's Tezzus, diamond*, 1300SAINT, Iyrus, Yume, Biggs Money and Lil Unky. Tickets run about $66 to $315 before fees.

That's the amphitheater date. This is the San Antonio date.

## The afterparty is the show

A flyer went up Monday from Lil Jay, the house promoter at 3|OAK on Live Oak Street, and Migo World. It's for the New Generation Tour official after party. Same night as the Austin show. Just not the same city. Young Thug, with the tour's YSL lineup on the bill. A commenter asked if Thug would actually perform, and Lil Jay answered with one word. The word was yes.

So on the night Austin gets the amphitheater, San Antonio gets the room, an hour and a half down 35 after the last song. General admission is $49.99 to $69.99 and VIP is $79.99 to $99.99, plus fees, through tixplug. Tables are by text. There wasn't a doors time out when this went up, and the venue's own calendar hadn't listed it yet either. So watch the promoter's page, not the bar's.

## The rest of the calendar at that address

3|OAK has a run going. DJ Pee Wee hosts an official after party on the 23rd. 2 Chainz hosts the 20+ Years of Carter after party on October 17, with a Halloween costume contest attached. Latto plays the room November 6, 18 and up. For a bar that isn't on anybody's touring map, that's four national names in seven weeks.

## Read it right

The main show is a Live Nation amphitheater with a pit and a policy. The after party is a club with a promoter and a flyer. They're two different nights with two different price tags, and the second one's where you'll be close enough to see the jewelry.
