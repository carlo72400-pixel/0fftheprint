Young Thug's New Generation Tour comes through Texas at the end of the month and San Antonio is not on it. Austin gets him on Wednesday 30 September at the Moody Amphitheater at Waterloo Park, doors at 6:30, show at 7, with Nav as the special guest and the YSL bench underneath: Tezzus, diamond*, 1300SAINT, Iyrus, Yume, Biggs Money and Lil Unky. Tickets run from about sixty six dollars to three hundred and fifteen before fees.

That is the arena date. This is the San Antonio date.

## The afterparty is the show

A flyer went up on Monday from Lil Jay, the house promoter at 3|OAK on Live Oak Street, and Migo World: the New Generation Tour official after party, same night, same city as the arena show is not. Young Thug, with the tour's YSL lineup on the bill. Lil Jay answered a commenter asking whether Thug would actually perform with one word, and the word was yes.

So on the night Austin gets the amphitheater, San Antonio gets the room, an hour and a half down 35 after the last song. General admission is $49.99 to $69.99 and VIP is $79.99 to $99.99 plus fees through tixplug. Tables are by text. No doors time had been published when this went up, and the venue's own calendar had not listed it yet either, so watch the promoter's page rather than the bar's.

## The rest of the calendar at that address

3|OAK has a run going. DJ Pee Wee hosts an official after party on the 23rd, 2 Chainz hosts the 20+ Years of Carter after party on 17 October with a Halloween costume contest attached, and Latto plays the room on 6 November, 18 and up. For a bar that is not on anybody's touring map, that is four national names in seven weeks.

## Read it right

The main show is a Live Nation amphitheater with a pit and a policy. The after party is a club with a promoter and a flyer. They are two different nights with two different price tags, and the second one is the one where you will be close enough to see the jewellery.
