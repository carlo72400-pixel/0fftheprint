San Antonio Plays At 11

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight Eastern on November 19, so San Antonio should get it at 11 the night before. Here's the clock, plus two rumors nobody's confirmed.
0TP-056  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  NOV 12, 2026

Ocean Drive by night in Miami Beach on July 25, 2016, by Gzzz, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0. Cropped and restyled.

Spotted: GTA 6 sitting locked on PS5s and Xboxes since November 10, up to 212 GB of it.

You've been waiting for years. It's one more week.

Here's the clock

Grand Theft Auto VI comes out Thursday, November 19, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version's been announced. Rockstar told the AP it's playable at midnight local time.

But the stores run on one clock. The US PlayStation Store and Microsoft's US store both list the release at 05:00 UTC. That's midnight in New York and 11 PM Wednesday in San Antonio. GameRant did the same math. Rockstar hasn't put an hour on it, so 11 is expected, not promised.

Cloud play on Xbox opens an hour later. That's midnight here.

The pre-load started Monday night for Xbox and Tuesday for PS5. Rockstar says it's up to 212 GB. Standard is $79.99. The Ultimate Edition is $99.99, and it's digital only.

Then tune the radio

Rockstar's announced six stations so far. One of them is Dirty South Classics, hosted by Trick and Trina. Rockstar only says Trick. HotNewHipHop and Revolt say it's Trick Daddy.

The preview runs David Banner's "Like A Pimp," and Lil' Flip is on it. Flip's from Cloverland, in Houston.

There's an album too. GTA VI: The Album is 34 new songs, made with Atlantic, out the same day. Travis Scott's on it, and he grew up in Missouri City. So is Yung Lean, on a song with Future and Metro Boomin.

What you heard and what's on the record

You heard reviews drop Wednesday morning. That's a leak. Insider Gaming reported November 18 off a leaker, and it says it checked. Rockstar hasn't confirmed a day or a time.

You heard Drake gets his own station. That goes back to Jermaine Dupri on a podcast in June 2025, and he said Grand Theft Auto, not VI. Nobody's confirmed it. Rockstar says more stations are coming, so it isn't a no either.

So mark your own calendar

Wednesday, November 18, 11 PM, the expected unlock. Thursday, November 19, 10 AM, Deftones go on sale. So that's a long night.

Saturday, November 21, Rockstar and PlayStation throw GTA VI: The Festival in Miami, with Travis Scott and Cardi B on the bill. That one's a flight.
