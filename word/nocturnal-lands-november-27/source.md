> Spotted: OsamaSon's Nocturnal, dated the day after Thanksgiving.

Thanksgiving is November 26. The album is November 27. Somebody already asked.

## The date on the page

HotNewHipHop's November list, posted November 2, puts OsamaSon's Nocturnal on November 27. Same line: JID's Live From NPR's Tiny Desk, and CZARFACE's Special Presentation.

Album of the Year has the same date. It's an LP, on Atlantic and Motion. No track list. No critic score yet. A comment under it is the word Thanksgiving and two question marks.

So it isn't Thanksgiving night. Neither page gives an hour. It's the day after. We'll call that the underground's Thanksgiving. The pages call it November 27.

## The first taste

The FADER, May 20. He put out "off that!" for his birthday, and The FADER called it possibly the first piece of Nocturnal. That's their hedge, not a track list. The track list is still blank.

Same post, the run up to it. Jump Out, then Psykotic, and a tour behind each. The FADER had Psykotic in its top albums of 2025.

## Texas already had him

Pitchfork, August 20, 2025, the Psykotic tour dates. November 26, 713 Music Hall, Houston. November 28, South Side Ballroom, Dallas. November 29, the Aztec Theatre, San Antonio. That's a year ago this month.
