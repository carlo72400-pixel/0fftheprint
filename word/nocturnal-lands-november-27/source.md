> Spotted: OsamaSon's Nocturnal, dated the day after Thanksgiving.

Thanksgiving is November 26. The album is November 27. Somebody already asked.

## The date on the page

HotNewHipHop's November list, posted November 2, puts OsamaSon's Nocturnal on November 27. Same line: JID's Live From NPR's Tiny Desk, and CZARFACE's Special Presentation.

Album of the Year has the same date. It's an LP, on Atlantic and Motion. No track list. No critic score yet. A comment under it is the word Thanksgiving and two question marks.

So it isn't Thanksgiving night. Neither page gives an hour. It's the day after. We'll call that the underground's Thanksgiving. The pages call it November 27.

## Where the name comes from

He is Amari Deshawn Adham Middleton, born May 20, 2003, in Columbus, Ohio, and raised in Goose Creek, South Carolina, and Columbus. Wikipedia says OsamaSon comes from his father's name, Osama. An uncle, a Geechee musician, taught him FL Studio. He started in 2020 as PradaUMari. By 2021 he was OsamaSon.

## The records before this one

Osama Season was July 21, 2023. Flex Musix, the debut studio album, was December 8, 2023, on Motion and Atlantic. Jump Out was January 24, 2025, 18 songs on Apple Music, Atlantic and Motion. Wikipedia says it was his first Billboard 200 album, peaking at number 151 on February 8. Psykotic was October 10, 2025, 17 tracks, with Che on it. Wikipedia's discography table has that one at number 81 on the Billboard 200 and number 20 on the R&B and hip-hop chart. Che was one of the seven names in [Seven Seats At The Back Of The List](https://0fftheprint.com/word/seven-seats-at-the-back-of-the-list/).

The FADER, on his 23rd birthday, May 20, 2026, called "off that!" possibly the first offering from Nocturnal. The track list is still blank.
