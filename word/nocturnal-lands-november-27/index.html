Nocturnal Lands November 27

OsamaSon's Nocturnal is dated November 27, the day after Thanksgiving. Bruiser Wolf and Conductor Williams are November 11. Westside Gunn and ZelooperZ are the 13th.
0TP-048  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  NOV 04, 2026

OsamaSon at Racket, New York, March 2, 2024, by Ninecalling, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0. Cropped.

Spotted: OsamaSon's Nocturnal, dated the day after Thanksgiving.

Thanksgiving is November 26. The album is November 27. Somebody already asked.

The date on the page

HotNewHipHop's November list, posted November 2, puts OsamaSon's Nocturnal on November 27. Same line: JID's Live From NPR's Tiny Desk, and CZARFACE's Special Presentation.

Album of the Year has the same date. It's an LP, on Atlantic and Motion. No track list. No critic score yet. A comment under it is the word Thanksgiving and two question marks.

So it isn't Thanksgiving night. Neither page gives an hour. It's the day after. We'll call that the underground's Thanksgiving. The pages call it November 27.

The first taste

The FADER, May 20. He put out "off that!" for his birthday, and The FADER called it possibly the first piece of Nocturnal. That's their hedge, not a track list. The track list is still blank.

Same post, the run up to it. Jump Out, then Psykotic, and a tour behind each. The FADER had Psykotic in its top albums of 2025.

Texas already had him

Pitchfork, August 20, 2025, the Psykotic tour dates. November 26, 713 Music Hall, Houston. November 28, South Side Ballroom, Dallas. November 29, the Aztec Theatre, San Antonio. That's a year ago this month.

The rest of the slate

November 11, a Wednesday. Bruiser Wolf and Conductor Williams, The Dealer Wins.

November 13, a Friday, is seven records deep on HotNewHipHop's list. We're watching two. ZelooperZ and Black Noi$e, Pinbreakers. Westside Gunn, HEELMATIC. HotNewHipHop also parks Offset's GMFU on that line, and says the original delay means you shouldn't bet the date.

November 19 on the same list is a GTA VI official soundtrack. The list doesn't say more than the title.

What we already wrote

The label that spent five years not wanting to be looked at took a convention floor. That's Opium Bought The Whole Convention. Seven names from the new underground were sitting in the back half of Complex's 100. That's Seven Seats At The Back Of The List. OsamaSon isn't on either page. November is a different list.

So mark your own calendar

November 11, November 13, November 27. None of these pages puts Nocturnal in San Antonio. The last date we can point to is the Aztec, November 29, 2025.

HotNewHipHop, Album of the Year, The FADER and Pitchfork. Wednesday, November 4, 2026.
