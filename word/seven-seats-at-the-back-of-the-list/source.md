> Spotted: seven names from the underground, sitting in the back half of Complex's 100.

You read lists from the top. We read them from the bottom.

Complex posted its 100 Hottest Rappers Right Now on 2 October and updated it on the 7th. Drake is number 1. Fine. Playboi Carti is 5. Yeat is 7. Ken Carson is 8. Nobody blinked.

Now go to the bottom.

## Count them

Tezzus at 92. Slayr at 90. diamond* at 89. Nine Vicious at 77. Pz' at 65. Molly Santana at 54. che at 43.

Seven names from the newest wave of the underground, on the same page as Jay-Z at 6 and Kendrick at 10. Complex calls che "one of the most consistent names in the underground." That is a big sentence on a list this crowded.

Then look at the release dates. On 25 September che and Tezzus put out RichPhonks. Two tracks. diamond* is on the first one, Gourmet. Three of the seven on one release, one week before the list went up. Two songs is not an album. It is a handshake on tape.

## But do not call it a crew

You heard they are all one camp. They are not. Tezzus is signed to Young Thug's YSL Records. Slayr tours under his own name. Complex ranked every one of them alone and never put them in a group.

We did. Because they keep landing on each other's songs, and the list just caught up to the songs.

## So they already came through Texas

Slayr's first headlining run, the Half-Blood Tour, played The Aztec Theatre in San Antonio on 13 August. Downtown, seven weeks before the list.

Young Thug toured this fall too. He called it The New Generation Tour, presented by YSL Records, and it played 713 Music Hall in Houston on 27 September. The man whose label signed Tezzus put the phrase on the marquee before Complex put the names on the page.

If you were at the Aztec in August, you saw one of the seven before the list did.

## The one who is missing

SK8STAR is not on it. Not at 100. Not anywhere. On 7 October he put out Peter Pusher, the first single off an album called AFTER THE GALA. No date yet. Lists like this get rewritten every month. Names go up. Names fall off.

So here is the only question about the November list. Who gets cut to make room for him?
