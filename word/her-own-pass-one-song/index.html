Her Own Pass, One Song

Kasane Teto's Fortnite pass went live Thursday, and she's on 1 of its 4 songs. Her biggest hits aren't in the game at all. Then on Sunday, there's a leek.
0TP-057  ·  0FF THE PRINT  ·  San Antonio  ·  NOV 12, 2026

Kasane Teto in the BIRDBRAIN music video, 2025, by Jamie Paige, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0. Cropped and restyled.

Spotted: Kasane Teto with her own Fortnite pass, live since Thursday morning.

Monday we told you she started as an April Fools joke. That's part one. This is the part where the joke gets a pickaxe.

Here's the pass

The update went live Thursday, November 12. Her pass is called TWINDRILL DIVA. It's 1,000 V-Bucks, or it comes with Fortnite Crew. You get Teto the second you buy it.

The pickaxe is Teto's Baguette Bonker. Her official bio says her favorite thing is French bread, so that tracks. The back bling is called Pearto. There are Miku and Teto slippers, an emote called BIRDBRAIN, and a style at the last tier that moves with the music.

But she's on one song

The pass has four Jam Tracks. Epic's own catalog credits Teto on one of them. It's "BIRDBRAIN," by Jamie Paige and OK Glass.

The other three are "Sa-Sen!" by MONT, Risa and Musashi, "Chronicles of Destiny" by The Home of Happy and James Corbin, and "Shut Up and Dance" by Walk the Moon.

That's the headliner's pass. Walk the Moon is in it.

So where are her hits

"Override," by Yoshida Yasei, came out November 29, 2023. It topped Billboard Japan's 2024 year-end Niconico Vocaloid chart, and it's at 114.9 million YouTube views. "Tetoris," by Hiiragi Magnetite, topped that chart for the first half of 2025. "Mesmerizer," by Satsuki, with Miku and Teto, is at 231.9 million.

None of them are in Fortnite. Epic's catalog has 763 Jam Tracks. Miku's credited on 7. Teto's credited on 1.

The bounce everybody knows is older than all of that. The song is "Ochame Kinō," by Lamaze-P, posted April 1, 2010. On May 10 he posted a video called Fukkireta. The next day came a version with Teto bobbing her head, and Know Your Meme counts more than 2,000 parodies in the first month.

What you heard and what's on the record

Monday we wrote that her shop items land Saturday. That's shakier than we made it sound. Two sites say Epic moved her out of that shop slot on November 4 and into the pass. One site still has her in the shop on the 14th. We can't read Epic's own post, so we're not betting on it. The Saturday shop date outlets tie to Epic is Cap'n Hatsune Miku's.

So mark your own calendar

Friday, 6 PM San Antonio time, the shop resets. Sunday, November 15, Miku and Teto take over Friendship Villa as captains. Epic's bringing a sprite called Mini Miku and a weapon called the Leekcapper. It's a leek.

Friendship Villa, Fortnite. Thursday, November 12, 2026.
