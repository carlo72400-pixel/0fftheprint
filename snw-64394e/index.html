      ★ ♥ ★

      Park

      Free, first come first served, lots open 8 AM. GA parks on the west side in the Wheels, Helmet, Pistons and Speedometer lots. Staff points you to a spot.

      1. I-35W north through Fort Worth. Stay in the regular lanes, TEXpress is a toll.
      2. Exit 69 (SH 114, Dallas / Bridgeport). Ride the frontage road, then left onto SH 114 West.
      3. Buc-ee's is right there on SH 114. Gas and food before you park, so you leave at midnight with a full tank.
      4. Right into the speedway at Jarrett Dr or Championship Pkwy, then around Victory Circle to the Foyt entry gate. Cops and staff wave you through.
      5. GA lots: Wheels, Helmet, Pistons, Speedometer, all on the west side by Gates 4, 5, 6. The Petty and Rutherford entry gates get you in too.
      6. Pin the car. Signal gets bad at midnight when the whole crowd is looking for their ride.
      Apple Maps Google Maps

      Rideshare drops and picks up on the east side: 17343 Mark Martin Dr, Fort Worth, TX 76177

      Box office (wristband problems, will call) is outside Gates 3 and 4. Saturday: 8 AM to end of show. Bring the Front Gate order number and ID.

      ADA parking is the Express Lot: Championship Pkwy, right on Victory Circle, past Allison Ave.

        At the gate

        Full pat-down and a metal detector. If they bounce something, it goes in the trash or back to the car.

        Cameras

        YesPhones.
        YesBasic point-and-shoots with a fixed lens and nothing attached.
        NoVideo cameras, GoPros and gimbal cams, plus their mounts.
        NoCameras with detachable lenses.
        NoDrones, selfie sticks, tripods, audio recorders.

        Fit check

        • No masks. Balaclavas and Halloween masks count.
        • No spiked jewelry. Spiked chokers, cuffs or belts are security's call.
        • No long chains, no wallet chains.
        • Paved lots, gravel and grass for 12 hours. Wear shoes you can still stand in at midnight.

        Inside

        The festival is in the infield. You walk in through the grandstand.

        Stages
        Four. Blue and Gold side by side at the north end, Diablo on the east side, Spiral at the south end.
        Meet spot
        Pick one together as soon as you get in, something on the map that won't move. Phones die and signal drops, so if you lose each other, go there at the next set change.
        Water
        Refill spots are the drop icons on the map. Screenshot it before you go.
        Food
        The festival has its own food and drink map (in Links).
        Lockers
        GA lockers from $32 online, more on site. A cable inside charges your phone. Good spot for jackets.
        Charging
        ChargeFuze stations, the lightning icons on the map.
        Medical
        Red cross icons. There's more than one tent.
        Lost stuff
        Guest Services booths, plus a digital lost and found (in Links).

        ★ ♥ ★

        Getting home

        Sleep in Fort Worth Saturday night. Getting out of the lot after midnight and then driving 4 or 5 hours on no sleep is the riskiest part of the whole day.

        Safest

        Room near the track

        Northlake, Roanoke or Alliance, a few miles from the lot. No highway after midnight. Priciest that night, so book something refundable now.

        Cheaper

        Room in Burleson

        37 miles down I-35W, about 45 min once the lot clears. Likely cheaper than rooms by the track that night, and Sunday's drive drops to about 4 hrs.

        Skip it

        Drive straight home

        Out of the lot around 1 AM, home around 6 AM, about 24 hours after you woke up.

        Split the driving both ways.
