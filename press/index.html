Credential requests

What we file. A frame gallery and a written piece, both published inside 24 hours of the show, on 0fftheprint.com under a catalogue number.

House rule. Subjects see the cut first. Off the record stays off the record. Unreleased footage, subject names and drop dates stay embargoed until the piece runs.

We work to the room’s rules. First three songs, no flash, escorted, no shooting from the seat, whatever the venue and the tour set. Tell us the restrictions and we follow them.

To reach us, email offtheprintcollective@gmail.com with the event and date. Publicists and venue press offices, this is the right address for credentials, review tickets, embargoed assets and corrections.

Corrections

If we got something wrong, email the address above and we will fix it on the page and say that we did. Cover photographs licensed from third parties are credited under the image with the photographer, the source and the licence, and every one of them is logged publicly in the repository as IMAGE-CREDITS.md.
