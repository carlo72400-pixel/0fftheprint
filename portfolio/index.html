Nights · Venue + Event

I shoot the room the way it felt

Club nights, bar nights, showcases, touring rooms, and the shops that keep the same street alive in the daytime. Compact rigs so nothing is in anybody's way, flash that does not flatten a face, and a grade built for that specific room. A red stage and a green dive bar are not the same problem, so they do not get the same look.

BLADE RAVE · PAPER TIGER · N ST MARY'S195 FRAMES · SET DELIVERED NEXT MORNING

ONE NIGHT, 195 stills and 32 clips, the edit in the venue's inbox the next morning. The club look was picked by measuring five rooms against five candidate grades, not by eye. Flash-lit portraits in the black room were split onto a second, quieter look so no face blows out and no corner turns to murk.

THE MIX · N ST MARY'S · UNDERGROUND SHOWCASE159 FRAMES · RING FLASH · 21 MIN SET

A dive bar lit almost entirely in neon and one green flood. 159 frames plus the full set on video, cut into a venue reel, a carousel, and a set of 3000px square album grabs pulled off the graded master so the artist can use them as cover art.

ZEN HAUS · THIRD FRIDAY NIGHT MARKET · DOWNTOWN SA301 FRAMES · 3 VERSIONS · PUBLIC GALLERY NEXT DAY

An outdoor market that opens in daylight and ends in the dark, so one night asks two different things of the camera. 301 frames, each one graded on its own instead of off a single preset, delivered in three versions: clean, black and white, and a print look. Vendors, crowd, and walk-up portraits, all on a compact rig so nobody had a lens in their way. The whole night is up as a public gallery so anyone who was there can find their own photo and save it full size.

LEGION INK · SMILEY ONERR · N NEW BRAUNFELS27 FRAMES · 3 LOOKS · SHOP + SIT DOWN

A working tattoo shop on North New Braunfels, shot in one afternoon while he was actually tattooing. 27 stills in three looks, plus the sit down interview on video. The room is small, lit by one window and the lamp over the chair, so the grade is solved per frame off the skin in that frame instead of off the room. The black and grey set is a real editorial monochrome, not a desaturation, because his line work reads harder without colour in the way.

THE MIX · RESIDENT EVIL VS SILENT HILL · HORROR NIGHT165 FRAMES · 3 LOOKS · PUBLIC GALLERY NEXT DAY

A horror themed night that lit itself in two halves, so the grade follows the room instead of flattening both into one house look. The red rooms keep their red while a compressor pulls saturation off everything except skin, so faces climb out of the wash. The fog rooms get ash desaturation and the haze is held down rather than lifted, so it reads as fog instead of milk. 165 frames, each solved for its own exposure and white balance off the skin in it, delivered in three looks. Nothing clipped, no frame with pure black in it, checked frame by frame rather than by eye. The selects are up as a public gallery.

ANDREW E. · STABLE HALL · WHOLESOME AGAIN WORLD TOUR196 FRAMES · 3 LOOKS · PROMOTER HAD IT NEXT DAY

A Filipino rap legend on a US tour date, covered on a media pass. 196 frames in three looks plus 33 vertical clips and a set of frame grabs, in the promoter's hands the next day. The stage is lit hard in red from one side and blue from the other at the same time, which is the case a single house look cannot solve, so every frame is solved for its own exposure and white balance off the skin in it. The LED wall behind him stays readable instead of blooming out into the faces in front of it. Promoter: REM Entertainment & Productions.

The morning after the show he sat down for a sit down at Sari-Sari, and the two jobs are one weekend of the same subject. 30 stills in three looks and 12:46 of interview, cut long for the client and into short single-question pieces like the three above. Two bodies rolling: one for picture, one parked for sound, because the better audio was not on the better camera. They landed on opposite sides of his eyeline, so cutting between them would have crossed the line, and the whole cut is built instead from one 4K angle reframed into three sizes with no upscaling. The cameras also disagreed about what time it was, so the sync was solved by measuring the two audio tracks against each other rather than trusting the file clocks. The question you see burned into each frame was asked off camera and stays on screen, so nobody has to be told what he is answering. Full set of six at 0FF THE PRINT.

CLOWN CHURCH · RAH RAH ROOM · MARKET, STAGE SHOW, LIVE BAND154 FRAMES · 3 LOOKS · 20 CLIPS

A DIY clown-themed vendor market that turned into a stage show and then a live band, in a room walled and lit almost entirely in red. Red is the strongest channel in 146 of the 154 frames, so warm tells you nothing here and the scenes are keyed on how much green and blue survive instead. The harder problem is that half the faces are white greasepaint, which a skin key built for warm skin will not find, so the target became a readability floor per face rather than a colour. 154 frames in three looks, 20 clips, and 510 frame grabs pulled at full resolution, plus 40 selects. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

SMILEY ONERR · LEGION INK · N NEW BRAUNFELSDAY ONE · 27 STILLS · 51 MIN ON TWO CAMERAS

A black and grey shop on N New Braunfels with burglar bars across the front, an Aztec calendar plate on one wall and a shelf of santos on another, so the room was always going to carry half of this. It is also a dark room with black walls, and black and grey ink goes green the second the white balance drifts, which is why nothing here got a batch preset. Day one was the room first and then the man working in it: 51 minutes on two cameras, the FX6 on the shop and the street and a compact rig for the vertical work, 36 clips and 27 stills, each frame graded on its own and delivered in three versions. The sit-down is its own day and it has not happened yet.
