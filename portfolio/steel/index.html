01 · Info

The operator

I am a director and colorist finishing my B.S. in Digital Cinematography at Full Sail, based in San Antonio. I write, direct, shoot, cut, and grade my own work, which means you deal with one person from the idea to the delivery.

My edge is color. I build my own looks as DCTLs in DaVinci Resolve, so nothing I hand you looks like a preset. My other edge is speed. I ran event coverage for the San Antonio Current as their one-person unit and turned edits around the same night, while the event was still warm.

Fun on set, serious in the timeline. Directing is the goal.
