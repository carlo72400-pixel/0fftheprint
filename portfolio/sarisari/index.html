A plan for the feed. Five minutes to read, and the only new idea in it is the one we already talked about at the counter.

01 · The problem

Food content is fine. It is also everywhere.

A food post sells a plate to somebody who already decided to eat.

It does not make anybody care, and caring is the thing that compounds. Every restaurant in this city posts the same close-up of the same fork pull. You said it yourself: it is good, and it is not hitting. It cannot. The category is full.

03 · The idea

Stop running a restaurant feed. Run the feed for Filipino San Antonio.

The restaurant is the room it all happens in.

Your own name already says it. A sari-sari store is the store the whole neighborhood passes through. The city drew a cultural heritage district around your corridor,9 and nobody covers it. There is no Filipino outlet in San Antonio. There is no competitor to beat. The chair is just empty, and you are the only ones old enough and big enough to sit in it.

04 · The four shelves

What actually gets posted.

THE COUNTER

one item · 45 seconds

The bakery case at ten in the morning. The snack aisle nobody outside the family can read. Somebody says what it is and who asked for it. The one people send to their friends.

THE TABLE

kamayan

Banana leaves the length of the table and no silverware. The most photogenic thing you do, and it is booking only, so every post is a booking ad that does not look like one.

THIRTY YEARS

the part nobody can copy

A market that started small. A kitchen that came after. A baker from the Philippines. A daughter running it now. One memory at a time, one post at a time.

THE BLOCK

everybody else

Other Filipino businesses on the corridor, in your feed, shot by me, nothing asked in return. This is the shelf that turns a restaurant account into an outlet.

05 · How it shoots

People do their work while they talk.

Never a sit-down against a wall. The case, the wok, the register, the loading door. Two cameras, one on the face, one on the hands, and the room light stays the room light. Nobody performs. People just do their job and answer questions while they do it.

One real episode a month. Three to five minutes, cut long for YouTube and sliced into four verticals.
Short pieces in between. Shot in batches, one morning at a time, so the feed never goes quiet.
You see everything first. Nothing posts that you have not watched.
