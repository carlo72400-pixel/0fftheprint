What it does: explains what this place is to somebody who has never walked in.

The 30 · shopping channel

SCSN: School of Comics Shopping Network

What it does: sells the comics wall, and says out loud that the artist keeps sixty percent.

The 15 · the quiet one

Made here

What it does: fifteen seconds. One hand, your logo, one stamp.

No stock footage. No fake set.
