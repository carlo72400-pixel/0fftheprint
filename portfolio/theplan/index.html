How you get robbed

The second a girl says out loud that she wants to model, a specific set of people show up. This isn't me being paranoid, it's the most predictable thing in this entire industry.

Money only ever moves toward you.
You never pay to model. Ever.
  • Not for classes. Not for onboarding, not for "training," not for a portfolio package, not for registration, not for a comp card they print. A real agency takes a commission out of jobs they get you. They make money when you make money. That's the only model that isn't a scam, and it holds even when the agency has a real office and a real sign.
  • Not for a "scout" who slid into your DMs. That is the number one scam aimed at exactly your profile. Real agencies have a public roster and a submissions page. Go to their website, look at who they represent, submit there yourself. Never the other direction.
  • Never "verify yourself" on a link. No legitimate company verifies you on a third-party page. That's a card harvest, every single time, no exceptions.
  • Never send back an overpayment. A "client" sends a check for more than the rate and asks you to return the difference. The check bounces a week later and you're out real money you already sent. This one gets people constantly.
  • Never meet a stranger private or at night. Video call first. Refusal to video call is the answer. First meet is public and daytime and somebody knows where you are. If a photographer won't do that, he isn't a photographer.
On the feet pics and the underwear, since you brought it up. I'm not going to moralize at you, it's your call and you're grown. But the practical case against it is that it's the single highest-scam-density corner of the internet. Fake buyers, payment reversed after you deliver, "verify with a gift card first," and the day it's out there you have zero control over who resells it or who it gets sent to. It also pays badly, because the supply is effectively infinite. Every lane in this document pays more per hour with none of that exposure. That's the whole argument. That's why I said none of that sad shit.
