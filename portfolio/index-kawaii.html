PULSE ANGELS 3 · 21ST STREET CO-OP · AUSTIN79 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS · INSTANT-FILM PRINTS

Fifteen DJs on three stages at an Austin co-op: the Commons, the dining room and the yard, each one lit a different color. One Luna Ultra from ten to half past midnight, then a ten-node tree in Resolve on every frame with a look built off the flyer, hot magenta, electric violet and ice blue on black, with skin handed back so faces still read as faces. 79 frames, then the whole set again as instant-film prints, the color done in Resolve and the paper built in Photoshop; nineteen of those prints went up on Instagram. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

SADBOYZ CLUB · THE WAV ROOM · ST. MARY'S STRIP220 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS · FROM THE RAW

In Heavy Rotation, the emo night at the Wav Room: Lil Peep, XXX, Bones, Lil Tracy and Yung Lean on the speakers, a red LED bar and blue LED couches. Every frame was developed from the RAW file instead of the camera JPEG, because the RAW keeps the brick and the dark corners the JPEG throws away. Two looks: the corrected night, and SADBOYZ CLUB, built off the flyer's cream paper, wine ink and rose, a Tumblr-era print with halation and grain made as real Photoshop layers and kept low. 220 frames, delivered the next morning. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

BRAINROT · NEW GUILD CO-OP · AUSTIN HOUSE SHOW202 FRAMES · 4 LOOKS · 8 CLIPS

A fundraiser house show at New Guild Co-op in Austin, and every room in the house was lit differently: tungsten living rooms, a magenta porch, a green dance floor and a flash-lit graffiti alley out back. The scene rules I brought in only knew blue over green, so every purple frame read as a dark room until magenta got its own rule, keyed on green going missing while red and blue stay alive. 202 frames in four looks and 8 clips: Pulp Fiction and Tenet, a Project X party look, and a Brainrot look whose false colour was sampled straight off the night's own flyer. The grain is a real Photoshop layer, measured so it never competes with detail. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

CLOWN CHURCH · RAH RAH ROOM · MARKET, STAGE SHOW, LIVE BAND154 FRAMES · 3 LOOKS · 20 CLIPS

A DIY clown-themed vendor market that turned into a stage show and then a live band, in a room walled and lit almost entirely in red. Red is the strongest channel in 146 of the 154 frames, so warm tells you nothing here and the scenes are keyed on how much green and blue survive instead. The harder problem is that half the faces are white greasepaint, which a skin key built for warm skin will not find, so the target became a readability floor per face rather than a colour. 154 frames in three looks, 20 clips, and 510 frame grabs pulled at full resolution, plus 40 selects. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

ANDREW E. · STABLE HALL · WHOLESOME AGAIN WORLD TOUR196 FRAMES · 3 LOOKS · PROMOTER HAD IT NEXT DAY

A Filipino rap legend on a US tour date, covered on a media pass. 196 frames in three looks plus 33 vertical clips and a set of frame grabs, in the promoter's hands the next day. The stage is lit hard in red from one side and blue from the other at the same time, which is the case a single house look cannot solve, so every frame is solved for its own exposure and white balance off the skin in it. The LED wall behind him stays readable instead of blooming out into the faces in front of it. Promoter: REM Entertainment & Productions.

BLADE RAVE · PAPER TIGER · N ST MARY'S195 FRAMES · SET DELIVERED NEXT MORNING

Flash-lit portraits in the black room were split onto a second, quieter look so no face blows out and no corner turns to murk.
