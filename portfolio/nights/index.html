The $15 Fill-a-Bag at both Austin stores in one morning, South Congress first and then Campus. The brief was no models: the clothes, the groups, and people showing off their bags and finds, so the shot list was built off the store's own Instagram first, the formats already pulling hundreds of thousands of plays there. The hard sun made the camera save its RAWs up to four stops dark, so every photo is the RAW and the camera's JPEG merged pixel by pixel: the camera's colour, the RAW's skin. 105 frames in two looks, 52 clips, three overheads of the piles cut out of a 360, and three edits the next day. PAIN is sixteen bars of PinkPantheress cut on the beat so it loops back into itself, with chapter cards lettered like Kill Bill; the other two run on the lav audio, captioned in the store's own reel type.

PULSE ANGELS 3 · 21ST STREET CO-OP · AUSTIN79 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS · INSTANT-FILM PRINTS

Fifteen DJs on three stages at an Austin co-op: the Commons, the dining room and the yard, each one lit a different color. One Luna Ultra from ten to half past midnight, then a ten-node tree in Resolve on every frame with a look built off the flyer, hot magenta, electric violet and ice blue on black, with skin handed back so faces still read as faces. 79 frames, then the whole set again as instant-film prints, the color done in Resolve and the paper built in Photoshop; nineteen of those prints went up on Instagram. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

WAP · PRIVATE PARK · SAN MARCOS45 FRAMES · 4 LOOKS

A Friday in a San Marcos backyard: string lights in the trees, eighteen and up, bring your own, a DIY slip n slide on the flyer, and the flash doing everything else. The camera shot RAW until half past ten and JPEG after that, so the night was graded as two cards, every RAW frame developed through the gain map the camera writes for it, on a tone curve fitted to this card because the usual model failed on it. 45 frames in four looks: CINEMATIC, the corrected night; WAP, the flyer's palette pushed; RISO 2-INK, every frame printed in pink and blue as if it came off a risograph; and FUNSAVER, a disposable camera with the orange date stamp in the corner. All four carry a real texture made in Photoshop as live High Pass layers, pulled back by half after the first pass put canvas weave on skin. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

HARAMBE 4 EVER · PAPER TIGER · SIDE STAGE62 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS · 3 CAMERAS

Four bands on the Paper Tiger side stage, Streetlamp, Midnight Dawn, Black Nail Polish and GABO, under one near-monochrome red wash all night, shot on three cameras: a Luna Ultra on the stage edge, an Ace Pro 2 in the pit rendered back to its framing from the RAW through the lens model stored in each file, and stills pulled from an X6 360. The ask was skin back under the red, so faces carry colour transferred from the night's own skin, measured on people standing outside the gel, and only faces: the room, the haze and the crowd stay as red as they were. 62 frames in two looks: SIDE STAGE, a restrained concert grade made for this room after all 59 cubes in the library were run on it, and HEAVEN LIGHT, the red re-voiced as the sunset gold on the flyer with a god-ray glow on the lights. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

CHILL & SMOKE · EXOTIC TAKEOUT · PERRIN BEITEL19 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS

A Sunday at a lounge on Perrin Beitel that dresses itself as a Chinese takeout: a lucky cat on the sign, red takeout boxes on the shelf, the name painted across the front. Shot from a quarter to four, while the room was still a shop, to the group photo out front at dusk. The look was printed on the flyer before anyone graded anything. 19 frames in two looks: corrected, and CHILL & SMOKE, built off the flyer's emerald, cream, takeout red and gold, with the plants keyed to the emerald, the whites capped so they print like cream paper, and the blacks kept the flyer's warm ink, because the first try tinted the shadows green and turned every black tee in the room teal. Every frame was developed from the RAW and colour denoised, since this camera's RAW carries about four times its own JPEG's shadow colour noise at every ISO. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

BAPTISM · THE DECO · GOTH RAVE60 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS

A goth rave at The Deco with a dress code of nuns, priests, angels and demons, in a room lit almost entirely red. Red LED is nearly a single channel, so every automatic tool fails at once: the white balance chases a wall, the face detector finds nothing and the meter darkens the person standing in front of the bright wall. Everything was developed from the RAW files on a hue-true curve fitted from this card, white balance read only off something neutral on the subject, and exposure moved on seventeen frames all night. 60 frames in two looks: corrected, and RELIQUARY, a silver gelatin plate with blood, rose and lapis inks laid back over it in Photoshop as real plates, because keyed per pixel the ink speckled on flash-lit skin. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

THIRD FRIDAY · ZEN HAUS · HISTORIC ST. PAUL SQUARE50 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS

The September Third Friday night market, which is in Historic St. Paul Square and not in La Villita whatever the internet says. A market at dusk is five lighting situations in twenty minutes: open sky, balcony shade, tungsten shopfronts, a magenta LED room and a blue one, plus thirteen frames the camera had already tone mapped locally, which no single curve can match, so those were tone mapped again by hand. 50 frames in two looks: corrected on matte uncoated paper, and HAUS INFRARED, colour infrared film thinking that prints every tree in the square crimson and pushes every cool tone that is not a gel to the Zen Haus cobalt, with skin, blonde hair and bulbs keyed out and green dyed hair put back by hand. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

BAR HOP · CLUB 212 + BAR HOUSE · RIVER WALK TO NORTH ALAMO39 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS · 2 CAMERAS

A Thursday that started at Club 212 on the River Walk, found the room dead and moved: the 212 rooftop, then Bar House on North Alamo, then a blue-LED club at ten to three. Nothing in a night like this repeats, so nothing was graded as a batch: every frame solved its own white balance off something neutral on the subject, exposure moved only where a face fell under a readability floor, and the seven long-exposure light paintings were left exactly as drawn because on a long exposure the trail is the photograph. 39 frames in two looks, from two cameras: corrected, and a Tarantino print picked for this night by a measured bakeoff of eleven candidates, on newsprint. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

SADBOYZ CLUB · THE WAV ROOM · ST. MARY'S STRIP220 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS · FROM THE RAW

In Heavy Rotation, the emo night at the Wav Room: Lil Peep, XXX, Bones, Lil Tracy and Yung Lean on the speakers, a red LED bar and blue LED couches. Every frame was developed from the RAW file instead of the camera JPEG, because the RAW keeps the brick and the dark corners the JPEG throws away. Two looks: the corrected night, and SADBOYZ CLUB, built off the flyer's cream paper, wine ink and rose, a Tumblr-era print with halation and grain made as real Photoshop layers and kept low. 220 frames, delivered the next morning. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

BURLESQUE NIGHT · RAH RAH ROOM · SAN PEDRO146 FRAMES · 204 GRABS · 4 LOOKS · 10 CLIPS

A vendor market under the skylights at five, then a burlesque show on a red velvet stage under a red wash, which is close to monochrome: on five of the eight stage clips the green and blue channels sit at one to five percent of the red. Readability was judged on the red channel alone, the only one carrying information, and the face detector was taught to ignore the painted portraits and dolls on the vendor tables. Every portrait got a retouch pass in Photoshop on real layers before the grain; the stage frames were left exactly as lit. Four looks: RED VELVET, the room's own print; Insomnia, the one cool grade of fifteen tried that clips nothing; and two dark prints made on purpose the morning after, OXBLOOD and OXWULF, the second crossing OXBLOOD with the orthochromatic look of Robert Eggers' Werwulf. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

MIGX · MI VAQUITA · RIVER WALK233 FRAMES · 5 LOOKS · 9 CLIPS

Flash portraits in a room with the biggest LED wall in the city and everyone in black: red neon on one side, blue and magenta LED on the other, hookah on the tables. The scene classifier keyed every frame on its LIGHT, not the clock, and the gel-lit frames had their starved channels rebuilt so faces stopped being silhouettes without the room losing its colour. Five looks: DEMON TIME, a custom print named off the club's own sign, plus Tenet and Pulp Fiction, then HELL THERMAL and BLACKLIGHT UV built in Photoshop as real layers, a solarised thermal ramp and a violet room where the whites glow. The grain is a real layer, measured so it never competes with detail. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

LUCHA NIGHT · EL LUCHADOR BAR · SOUTHTOWN15 STILLS · 87 GRABS · 3 LOOKS · 12 CLIPS

An outdoor ring in the backyard of a lucha libre bar, tungsten string lights and floods, no gels and no flash. Two matches in fifty minutes on the Luna Ultra with a GO Ultra clipped in at ringside. The video was pulled for its best frames the same way the stills were scored, one per moment, so the grabs sit with the photographs. Three looks: a custom Lucha Night print, tungsten gold with the red masks pushed; Reservoir Dogs, the truest colour of the three; and Death Proof, a grindhouse print with the greens muted. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

BRAINROT · NEW GUILD CO-OP · AUSTIN HOUSE SHOW202 FRAMES · 4 LOOKS · 8 CLIPS

A fundraiser house show at New Guild Co-op in Austin, and every room in the house was lit differently: tungsten living rooms, a magenta porch, a green dance floor and a flash-lit graffiti alley out back. The scene rules I brought in only knew blue over green, so every purple frame read as a dark room until magenta got its own rule, keyed on green going missing while red and blue stay alive. 202 frames in four looks and 8 clips: Pulp Fiction and Tenet, a Project X party look, and a Brainrot look whose false colour was sampled straight off the night's own flyer. The grain is a real Photoshop layer, measured so it never competes with detail. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

MIKE DIMES · SLACKERS · B.I.L.L.Y RELEASE PARTY121 FRAMES · 2 LOOKS · 10 CLIPS

The night before B.I.L.L.Y dropped, in a sports and arcade bar lit by cabinet screens and fifteen televisions, which means the key light changed colour frame to frame. On-camera flash reaches whoever stands closest and falls off behind them, so every face was relit on its own, the dim ones brought up to match and the lit ones left alone, and the frames lit by a single arcade colour had their dead channels rebuilt so nobody stayed a silhouette. 121 frames in two looks and 10 clips: Jackie Brown as the clean hero print, Kill Bill Vol. 1 as the loud one, both calibrated against 465 measured pairs. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

CLOWN CHURCH · RAH RAH ROOM · MARKET, STAGE SHOW, LIVE BAND154 FRAMES · 3 LOOKS · 20 CLIPS

A DIY clown-themed vendor market that turned into a stage show and then a live band, in a room walled and lit almost entirely in red. Red is the strongest channel in 146 of the 154 frames, so warm tells you nothing here and the scenes are keyed on how much green and blue survive instead. The harder problem is that half the faces are white greasepaint, which a skin key built for warm skin will not find, so the target became a readability floor per face rather than a colour. 154 frames in three looks, 20 clips, and 510 frame grabs pulled at full resolution, plus 40 selects. The public gallery is up at 0FF THE PRINT.

The morning after the show he sat down for a sit down at Sari-Sari, and the two jobs are one weekend of the same subject. 30 stills in three looks and 12:46 of interview, cut long for the client and into short single-question pieces like the three above. Two bodies rolling: one for picture, one parked for sound, because the better audio was not on the better camera. They landed on opposite sides of his eyeline, so cutting between them would have crossed the line, and the whole cut is built instead from one 4K angle reframed into three sizes with no upscaling. The cameras also disagreed about what time it was, so the sync was solved by measuring the two audio tracks against each other rather than trusting the file clocks. The question you see burned into each frame was asked off camera and stays on screen, so nobody has to be told what he is answering. Full set of six at 0FF THE PRINT.

THE MIX · RESIDENT EVIL VS SILENT HILL · HORROR NIGHT165 FRAMES · 3 LOOKS · PUBLIC GALLERY NEXT DAY

A horror themed night that lit itself in two halves, so the grade follows the room instead of flattening both into one house look. The red rooms keep their red while a compressor pulls saturation off everything except skin, so faces climb out of the wash. The fog rooms get ash desaturation and the haze is held down rather than lifted, so it reads as fog instead of milk. 165 frames, each solved for its own exposure and white balance off the skin in it, delivered in three looks. Nothing clipped, no frame with pure black in it, checked frame by frame rather than by eye. The selects are up as a public gallery.

LEGION INK · SMILEY ONERR · N NEW BRAUNFELS27 FRAMES · 3 LOOKS · SHOP + SIT DOWN

A working tattoo shop on North New Braunfels, shot in one afternoon while he was actually tattooing. 27 stills in three looks, plus the sit down interview on video. The room is small, lit by one window and the lamp over the chair, so the grade is solved per frame off the skin in that frame instead of off the room. The black and grey set is a real editorial monochrome, not a desaturation, because his line work reads harder without colour in the way.
