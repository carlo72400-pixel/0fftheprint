On the fee going up: the "it jumps September 1" line going around traces to one unattributed blog post, and SXSW publishes no fee calendar. But the tiers are real — it went $35 to $75 in each of the last two cycles, on a date they never announce. It is $35 today. Apply while $35 exists.

Verified against sxsw.com and cart.sxsw.com, Aug 29 2026.

What the application actually weighs, in their order

  • 1. Where the music has been featured. Playlists, blogs, magazines, film, games. SXSW says explicitly it does not have to be Spotify editorial — user-made playlists count. Sit down and list every single one. This is the field distribution unlocks.
  • 2. Current developments. Their words: "there should be a reason for the artist to be here other than simply checking off a box." This is where SIDE B goes. Unreleased, already teased on your own grid, arriving in 2027. The showcase is the launch. That's a real answer and it's true.
  • 3. The bio field. They ask for thorough. Houston-born, San Antonio-based, the room at The Mix, the visual side.
  • 4. Social channels, to see how you engage a fanbase. See section 02.

Judged on originality, technical ability, songwriting and career trajectory, and weighted on social presence, streaming numbers, show experience and release timeline. They also say "not every artist needs to be a shiny finished article."

Odds, honestly: in 2024 about 1,248 acts played out of roughly 7,009 applicants — near 18%, far better than the 2% folklore, but that pool includes label-backed acts. Unsigned, 1.2K followers, no streaming history, you're under it. Build this so a December 18 no changes nothing.

Don't spend a dollar driving to Atlanta before SIDE B ships and the streaming links exist. 988 miles, about 14 hours each way. Nobody in Atlanta pays a 1.2K-follower out-of-state artist. Every realistic slot in the 250–300 cap tier is unpaid support. So budget it as a production, not a tour. What $460 buys is four dated live credits for the SXSW bio field and four cities of footage. Target a Thursday–Sunday in late October or November, after the ACL weekends clear. Line up couches in all three away cities before committing. If this gets written down as a money-maker it gets abandoned in week two.
