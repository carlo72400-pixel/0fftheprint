How it's shot

  • Vertical 9:16 in 4K, 30 frames a second. Horizontal only where you ask for it.
  • Story clips in normal color so they post straight off the phone. Everything else gets graded.
  • Inside, the shutter is matched to the store lights so nothing flickers on a phone screen.
  • Nothing burned in, no music on the files. Your team picks the sound.
  • Faces and product stay out of the top and bottom of the frame, where Instagram puts its buttons.

People on camera

  • Nobody gets featured without saying yes first.
  • Kids stay out of the featured shots.
  • Anyone in a clip that runs as a paid ad signs a one-page release. If you have your own, we use yours.

What I need from you

  1. What time you want me there, and if Campus first works.
  2. Who I check in with at each store.
  3. Who gets the Story clips during the sale.
  4. Raw clips or edited reels, and where the files go.
  5. Which account each one's for: Campus, South Congress, or both.
  6. Your must-have shots, and whether any of it runs as a paid ad.
