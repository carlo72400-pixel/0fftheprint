How it's shot

  • Vertical 9:16 in 4K, 30 frames a second. Horizontal only where you ask for it.
  • Shutter matched to the store lights so nothing flickers on a phone screen.
  • Nothing burned in, no music on the files. Your team picks the sound.
  • Faces and product stay out of the top and bottom of the frame, where Instagram puts its buttons.

People on camera

  • Nobody gets featured without saying yes first.
  • Kids stay out of the featured shots.
  • Anyone in a clip that runs as a paid ad signs a one-page release. If you have your own, we use yours.

What I need from you

  1. Which store, and who's my contact there that morning.
  2. Your must-have shots, if there's anything missing here.
  3. Logo and brand colors, if you want them on the reel.
  4. Which account it's for: the Austin page, the main page, or both.
  5. Whether any of it runs as a paid ad.
  6. Where you want the files, and who signs off on them.

Totally fine to cut anything that doesn't fit the day. Easier to cross shots off than to wish we had them.
