## 1. San Antonio venues by tier: operators, bookers, media and photo policies

### Takeaway
Each large SA room has a different gatekeeper:
- **Frost Bank Center:** OVG books it.
- **Boeing Center at Tech Port:** ASM Global manages it and Tobin Entertainment books it.
- **Aztec Theatre:** Live Nation is the listed operator.
- **Stable Hall:** Dayglo Presents took over booking in Nov 2025, amid a lawsuit with the previous operator.

Below them is an active independent tier the shooter is not yet working: Vibes Event Center and Vibes Underground, The Rock Box, The Starlighter, Deco Ballroom, 502 Bar, Qulturè and Studio 210. The St. Mary's Strip keeps turning over: the Amp Room closed in Oct 2025, and La Descarga is expected in 2027. No SA venue press or photo-policy page could be verified.

## 2. Austin venues by tier: operators, bookers, media policies

### Takeaway
Austin's club tier is contracting fast:
- Empire Control Room & Garage closed in 2026, and Marlow, Koriente and Feels So Good are gone.
- Valhalla closes Oct 26, 2026.
- The building housing Elysium and The 13th Floor is for sale.

Meanwhile, large electronic rooms (Kingdom, Concourse Project) and independents (Mohawk, Hole in the Wall, Come and Take It Live) keep full calendars. ACL Live's parent company is exploring a sale, and the COTA amphitheater's naming deal expires around the end of 2026.

## 6. City and state resources: programs, directories, grants, film offices, networking

### Takeaway
The only near-term deadline is Austin's Nexus grant, whose fall round closes **Oct 13, 2026** ($5K or $10K; residency rules unverified). San Antonio's Artist Grants ($7,500 or $15,000, explicitly including filmmakers and media artists) have opened in January in past cycles. Free visibility comes from the Texas Music Industry Directory (it has freelance-journalist and video-production listings) and the official city calendars (GetCreativeSA, Create Austin). CreativeMornings San Antonio appears dormant.

### Cited Findings

**City of San Antonio: Department of Arts & Culture (DAC)**

  - FY2026 funded 54 artists at **$7,500 or $15,000** each (announced late Sept 2025), for new work that premieres publicly within 18 months.
