Rules of the look

  • Four colors max. The whole identity prints on a toner budget, which is the point. The stamp red already matches his red-on-black logo, so the file sits under his existing brand instead of fighting it.
  • Type: the typewriter (Special Elite), the stencil (Allerta), the handler's pen (marker), and real cut letters, made by hand and scanned once.
  • Cover art gets shot, not generated: a real manila folder, or a reel-to-reel tape box stenciled GATEWAY SESSION TAPES, paperclipped portrait, scanned dirty. Stamps land in Photoshop. Singles reuse inner pages so the catalog reads as one file on the DSP grid.
  • No real agency seals on props or merch (federal seal rules are a real thing). Stamps and redactions carry the look without them.

The Machine

four repeatable formats. nothing one-off.

F1 · THE FILES weekly carousel

Scanned pages from the subject file, one release a week, each with one redacted line for people to argue about:

  • FILE 001, INTAKE. Intake form, Zener score sheet reading 20/25 with "chance is 5" circled, handler note: "subject hums during testing. recommend suppression."
  • FILE 002, INCIDENT. Suppression session blackout. Every recorder returned static except audio channel 2, which returned music. Recommend termination of study.
  • FILE 003, CLOSURE. Program shutdown memo, subjects released. Final line unredacted: SUBJECT V. STATUS: AT LARGE. NEXT TRANSMISSION 09.29.26.
  • Every file carries one KNOWN ASSOCIATES page: the credits list with names redacted and handles legible. The flex does the marketing on its own.

F2 · THE FOOTAGE 15-30s recovered tape

CCTV high angle, timecode burn, nightvision green. Lights fail when he walks past. The camera glitches when it lands on his face. And in one tape he is on the wrong side of the glass, running the console himself: the subject became the engineer. Graded and broken with the VP packs.

F3 · THE BROADCAST interrupt clips

A slate cuts in mid-scroll: WE INTERRUPT THIS PROGRAM. Our own alarm tone from the sound bank, not the real EAS tones (those get clips pulled). Snippet hits over static. Final week these become the nightly countdown.

F4 · THE REAL the human layer

Studio clips, console hands, live sets, the homies. The lore never touches this layer: file world on the grid, real world in between. That contrast is what makes both land.

Cadence floor from Sept 7: three posts a week. One FILE or BROADCAST, one FOOTAGE, one REAL. Everything cut in batches on shoot days, nothing made day-of.
