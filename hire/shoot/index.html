04DON'T MISS

The five ways this goes wrong
  • You start groups late and finish in the dark. 7:00 sharp.
  • Somebody leaves before the big group. That's why it's right after the prayer.
  • Cake happens and nobody told you. Ask when you walk in.
  • Prayer starts and the mic isn't on. Clip it early and let it roll.
  • 400 candids and six group frames. The groups are what he paid for.

06SUNDAY

Read before you start cutting
  • Insta360 writes no orientation flag. Fix rotation with the YuNet pass, never Haar.
  • Luna Ultra video is flat but tagged bt709 while the stills are normal. De-log uniformly, never per-clip auto-levels.
  • The DNG ignores digital zoom, so RAW is a wider, differently framed photo. Grade the JPG.
  • Dark room, brown skin: open the subject on the source first, gamma not lift. Subjects never blow out, background never goes void.
  • Run it through shoot-delivery-engine, per-photo grades, not a preset.
