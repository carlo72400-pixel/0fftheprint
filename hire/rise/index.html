The Honest Part

what ACL actually is right now

Door closed · 2026 lineup

ACL Fest 2026 is Oct 2–4 + 9–11 at Zilker, 25th anniversary, and the poster has been printed since spring: Charli XCX, Lorde, Twenty One Pilots, The xx, Kings of Leon. Nobody joins that lineup six weeks out. Chasing it in 2026 is chasing air.

Receipts: aclfestival.com

Door open · ACL weekend

Those two weekends put six figures of music fans in Austin, and most of the city's stages are not Zilker. Three ways in, all real:

  • Fest Nights + aftershow bills (Oct 2–13). Emo's, Stubb's, Scoot Inn, Empire, Parish, Antone's. Venues and promoters fill local support slots on those bills. We pitch now, we take any slot.
  • The unofficial layer. Hotel Vegas, Cheer Up Charlies, Chess Club, Swan Dive, Flamingo Cantina, house shows. Every room in Austin programs those weekends and support falls through late. Being ready on 48 hours notice is the skill.
  • The street. No permission required. Pop-up sets and two cameras in the ACL flow. The takeover gets filmed whether anyone books us or not.
Door wide open · the official one

SXSW 2027, Austin, Mar 15–21. Applications are open RIGHT NOW. Deadline Nov 20 2026, answers by Dec 18. Unsigned artists eligible. This is the official Texas festival slot with our name available on it, and it is won in September, not November.

ACL official is a 2027 campaign: Austin draw receipts + SXSW credit + being known to the aftershow promoters is what a real case looks like. We build it this fall.

Apply: sxsw.com/apply
